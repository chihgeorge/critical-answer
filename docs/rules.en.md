# Full rules

The README's "rules in one minute" section is the quick reference; this is the full version. For how the host runs the game, see the [hosting guide](hosting.en.md).

## Turns and question banks

**Initiative roll**: after the stone doors open at the start, each team presses 「🎲 擲骰」 (roll) on its phone to roll a d20. The big screen lines up every team's die, and the highest roll goes first. The room rerolls tied teams for them until the tie is broken. A team that has not rolled 15 seconds after the start (an offline one included) is rolled for by the room; an offline team gets the full 15 seconds too, and can still roll if its phone comes back in time. The host page has 「🎲 全部代擲」 (roll for everyone), which rolls for every team that has not rolled yet. After the order is set, the big screen holds it for about 4 seconds (2.5 seconds more for each reroll), then the first team starts answering, and the opening narration introduces the teams in this order.

**Global turns**: all teams answer in turn, in the order the initiative roll set, which stays the same all game. When a team's turn comes, the big screen shows the question, its difficulty and the monster the team faces. Questions are split into five banks by difficulty 1–5. Each room draws from the bank of its own difficulty. All teams share one deck, so two teams never get the same question at the same time. The order of multiple-choice options is shuffled again at the start of every game. So are the items of a sort question, and they never come up in the correct order.

## Judging

The host reads the question out (the question is on the big screen; the answer is only on the host's phone). The team types its answer on a phone (fill-in questions), and the host judges it with buttons. When hosting from a laptop, the host can also use the keyboard: `1` correct, `2` wrong and retry, `3` wrong and flee; after the answer is revealed, `1` or Enter goes to the next question (for a monster with a dish, the first press is 「🍲 上菜」 (serve), the next one goes on). These keys do nothing while the pick list of 「指定下一題」 (pick next question) or `⚙ 更多` (more) is open.
- **Correct**: the team defeats this room's monster, gets its **reward points** (by default rooms 1–8 give 10/20/20/30/40/40/50/50), and moves on to the next room.
- **Wrong, retry**: the team stays in the same room and answers the same question again.
- **Wrong, flee**: the team stays in the same room, facing the same monster, but gets a new question from the bank one level lower, difficulty −1 (the reward points still follow this monster; nothing is deducted).
- **Change to correct**: when the host presses 「答錯」 (wrong) on a fill-in question by mistake, 「↩ 剛才的{隊名}改判答對」 (change {team}'s answer to correct) shows below the judge buttons (keyboard `0`). Pressing it counts that question as correct: score, reward points, dish and narration all go as for a correct answer, and a cleric's wait (with the retry or flee chosen after it) is taken back too. It works only until the game's next move: once the next team submits an answer, locks, rolls or uses a class ability, the host judges anything else, picks a next question or sends a team to the boss room, or the cleric prays, the button goes away. A phone disconnecting or reconnecting, or a team tapping an option (before locking), is not a move. The button cannot be pressed for half a second after it shows up, so a double press on 「答錯」 cannot land on it.

## Multiple-choice questions

**Multiple-choice questions** need no judging from the host: the team whose turn it is taps one option on its phone, then presses 「鎖定答案」 (lock answer). The result is revealed only after the host presses 「公布答案」 (reveal answer, keyboard `1`), so fill-in and multiple-choice questions have the same pace (design in [docs/designs/host-reveal.md](designs/host-reveal.md)). This team's phone shows the text of the question in a dark bar above the options (for an image or video question, its 題幹 (prompt), if it has one); it stays there through a wrong answer, a retry and the reveal of a correct answer. For image questions, the image sits right below 「輪到我們了！」 (our turn!). A video plays only on the big screen: in that spot the phone shows 「▶ 看大螢幕」 (watch the big screen), or 「🎵 聽大螢幕」 (listen to the big screen) for a music question. On a **fill-in question**, this team's phone also shows the question and an input box: type one answer (at most 40 characters) and press 「送出」 (submit). The host page shows 「隊伍答：…」 (team answered: …) below the question (「（還沒送出）」 (not submitted yet) before a submit), and the big screen shows 「⚔️ {隊名}：{答案}」 (team name: answer). Fill-in questions are still judged by the host, and the host can judge before anything is submitted (if a phone has trouble, the team can say the answer to the host). After a submit, it stays this team's turn until the host judges, even if the phone sleeps or disconnects. Other teams' phones do not show the question. After 「公布答案」 on a multiple-choice question, the big screen pauses 2 seconds, then reveals right or wrong. On a correct answer, a **crit die** (d20) is rolled next: the big screen dims, the die rolls for 2 seconds and stops on a number, the phone also shows the number rolled, and only then is the score counted. A 20 (critical success) gives an extra half of this question's reward points (the thief's sneak attack ×1.5 does not raise this half), and in the boss room the boss loses one extra heart. A 1 (critical failure) still counts as correct; only the narration and sound are different. If the phone disconnects during these 2 seconds of rolling, the team's correct answer still counts; the team is shown as offline only after the die stops. A correct fill-in answer does not roll the crit die. On a wrong answer, the big screen marks only the chosen wrong option in red; the correct answer is not revealed, and the team chooses 「重答」 (retry) on its phone (the wrong option turns grey and cannot be chosen again) or 「逃跑」 (flee). After a correct answer, the answer stays on the big screen, and the next question comes only when the host presses 「下一題」 (next question). If a team has not locked an answer in 15 seconds (or, after a wrong answer, has not chosen retry or flee in 15 seconds), the host page shows 「替{隊名}選・重答／逃跑」 (choose for {team}: retry / flee), with keyboard `2` and `3` for these two buttons. On a multiple-choice question, `1` acts as 「公布答案」 only after the team has locked an answer. The boss room is an exception: after a wrong answer there is no retry-or-flee choice. The red option stays 2 seconds, then the boss automatically changes the question and the turn passes to the next team (the phone shows 「首領要換題了 ▶」 (the boss is changing the question)), so the next team never gets a question with options already removed. The host page's stuck row shows only 「重答」, and keyboard `3` does nothing. Design in [docs/designs/phone-answering.md](designs/phone-answering.md) (for boss-room rules see 「首領房規則（v0.15）」 (boss room rules, v0.15)); for the question on the phone see [docs/designs/phone-question-text.md](designs/phone-question-text.md).

## Sort questions

A **sort question** gives four items and a rule to sort them by (for example 「依年齡由大到小排」 (oldest to youngest)); the rule is written in the question. The team whose turn it is holds the ☰ on the right of an item on its phone and drags it up or down to put the items in order (only the ☰ drags; anywhere else the page scrolls as usual; with a keyboard, focus the ☰ and press the up or down arrow), then presses 「鎖定答案」 (lock answer). From there it works like a multiple-choice question: the host presses 「公布答案」 (reveal answer), and the big screen pauses 2 seconds before the reveal. Only all four places right counts as correct; there are no partial points. A correct answer rolls the crit die, and the big screen and phone show the correct order (✓ on every place). On a wrong answer, the big screen and phone mark only whether each place in the team's order is right (✓, green) or wrong (✕, red); the correct order is not revealed, and the team chooses retry or flee. On a retry, the phone keeps the last order, so the team only fixes the wrong places; the same order cannot be locked twice. Retry, flee, the boss room's question change, the host's stuck row and the warrior's, thief's and cleric's abilities all work as for multiple choice; on a sort question the mage shows 「🔮 第 1 個：…」 (first item: …) (see [Classes](#classes)). The host page shows the correct order below the question (「答案（只有你看得到）」 (answer, only you can see it)), and the host cannot press 「答對」 (correct).

## Persuasion check

The input box of a fill-in question starts locked; the team whose turn it is must first press 「🎲 擲骰說服」 (roll to persuade) on its phone. The room rolls 1d20 plus the class bonus (thief +4, cleric +3, mage +2, chef +1, no class +1, warrior +0) against the monster's DC: difficulty 1–5 are 8/10/12/14/16 in order, and the boss is 16. The big screen shows 「🗣️ 說服 DC {DC}（+{加值}）」 (persuade DC {DC} (+{bonus})); the roll dims the screen and the die rolls for 2 seconds, as before. On a pass, both the phone and the big screen show 「🔮 第一個字：…」 (first character: …) (there is no hint when the answer is only one letter or digit, and the check does not give it again when the mage has already given the first character on this question). On a fail, the team just answers. A 20 is a persuasion success: no answer is needed and it counts as correct. A 1 is a persuasion failure: a correct answer on this question gets only half the base reward points (the thief's sneak attack still gives ×1.5, and a warrior's correct roll is halved too). Each team rolls only once per question: a retry does not roll again; fleeing to a new question rolls again. Until the check is rolled, the room accepts no typed answer, and the warrior cannot roll instead of answering; the host can still judge directly. If the phone disconnects during the 2 seconds of rolling, it is still this team's turn. If the room happens to restart during the 2 seconds of a 20, the persuasion success still counts when the team reconnects and it is its turn again (after any judging, reveal or phase transition in progress ends). A pass and a fail each have a sound, and the narration has lines for a 20 and a 1. When a difficulty or the boss has no fill-in questions in the bank, there is no check there, and the bank editor warns about it.

## Progress points and the boss room

Each correct answer also adds **progress points** (equal to the current question's difficulty 1–5; fleeing makes the difficulty −1). A team that reaches **15** progress points enters the boss room; a team that passes the last room with fewer than 15 points stays in the last room and keeps fighting. As soon as one team is in the boss room, every other team goes straight to the boss room on its next correct answer. Only the host can see progress points (the host page shows 「進度/15」 (progress/15) for each team); the phones and the big screen show only reward points. The host can also send a stuck team straight to the boss room from 「⚙ 更多」. Next to each team on the host page is 「指定下一題」 (pick next question): the host can pick this team's next question from the bank of its next room's difficulty (questions whose answer has been revealed, that another team is answering, or that are already queued for another team cannot be picked). The next time this team moves to a question of that difficulty, it gets this question. When a team is in the boss room, the host can also pick the boss room's next question (when the boss has phases, only the current phase's questions are listed). The boss's health is shared, and teams take turns attacking.

## Boss phases

A bank can split the boss fight into phases, each with its own name, rules, health bar and questions. When a phase's health runs out (or all its questions are used), the boss does not fall: the big screen reveals the answer as usual, and after the host presses 「下一題」 a 「還沒結束！」 (not over yet!) transition card appears with the new phase's name and rules; the boss changes color and its hearts fill back up one by one. Press 「下一題」 again to go on. Players do not know in advance how many phases there are; only the host page shows 「第 n/N 階段」 (phase n/N). Design in [docs/designs/boss-phases.md](designs/boss-phases.md).

## Boss scoring

Each hit on the boss gives **10** reward points; the **finishing blow** (the hit that brings the last phase's health to 0) adds 4 D20 (4–80 points). When the crit die rolls a 20, this hit gets an extra half (5 points by default) and the boss loses one extra heart; the 4 D20 of the finishing blow do not change. The big screen rolls those 4 dice first (they land one by one); the points are added, and the podium shows, only after they stop.

## Winning and playing again

The team with the highest total reward points wins. When a tie changes the top three, each tied team rolls a d20; the higher roll goes first, and teams still tied roll again until it is settled (ties from fourth place down are not rolled). Before the scores show, the narration names 「本場大成功王」 (this game's critical-success king: the team that rolled the most natural 20s; skipped when nobody rolled a 20), then calls the places from third to first, and the big screen fills the podium one place at a time; when dice settle a tie, the big screen rolls them first for everyone to see. The scores, the crown and the epilogue show only once first place is called, and only then do the phones show the places. Design in [docs/designs/podium-reveal.md](designs/podium-reveal.md). Defeating the boss only ends the game; it does not make that team the champion. The end screen names separately who landed the last blow. The host can also end early from 「⚙ 更多」 with 「結束冒險」 (end adventure) (then press 「確定結束？」 (really end?) once more): teams are ranked by their current reward points, with the same podium and epilogue, but there is no last blow, and the epilogue does not say the boss was defeated. Design in [docs/designs/scoring-and-score-pools.md](designs/scoring-and-score-pools.md). After the game ends, the host page has 「回到大廳，再玩一場」 (back to the lobby, play again) (only the host can see it): the phones, host page and big screen all go back to the lobby. Teams stay the same; team names, classes (which can be chosen again in the lobby), the join password and AI narration settings are kept; reward points and progress go back to zero. The next game first uses questions whose answers were not revealed in the last game, and only after those run out uses questions whose answers were revealed (boss phases too).

## Classes

In the lobby, each team can choose one class on a phone, or choose 「不選（普通冒險者）」 (none, ordinary adventurer). Any phone of the team can choose; the last choice before the start counts, and the class is fixed after the start (it can be chosen again when going back to the lobby to play again). The host page shows each team's class and its uses left (the chef is passive and shows no uses). The warrior and the mage have 4 uses per game; the cleric and the thief have 2. All five classes work. The mage removes 2 wrong options on a multiple-choice question; on a sort question, the mage lets the phone, host page and big screen see 「🔮 第 1 個：…」 (first item: the first item of the correct order, once per question); on a fill-in question, the mage lets the phone, host page and big screen see 「🔮 第一個字：…」 (the first letter or digit of the answer, skipping spaces and punctuation; once per question; it cannot be used, and no use is spent, when the answer is only one letter or digit or when this team's persuasion check has already given the first character). The cleric prays after a wrong answer to answer again this turn (on a fill-in question, after the host presses 「答錯」 (wrong, keyboard `2`), both the phone and the host page show three buttons: pray, retry, flee (host page keyboard `2`, `3`, `4`); whoever presses first counts). The thief presses 「偷襲」 (sneak attack) before answering: a correct answer on this question gives ×1.5 reward points (rounded half up, the boss's final 4d20 too) and ×2 boss damage; a wrong answer loses it. The chef's ability is passive, with nothing to press: every reward gain (飽足感) is 15% bigger, rounded half up and at least +1, taken on the whole gain (after the sneak attack, a critical success and the boss's 4d20); the big screen's dish card and the phone's correct line show 「（廚師 +N）」 (chef +N). When it is the warrior's turn, the phone shows 「🎲 擲骰代替答題」 (roll instead of answering) below 「鎖定答案」 (on a fill-in question it is below 「送出」, and the persuasion check must be rolled first): the room rolls 1d20 at once, the big screen dims, and the die rolls for 2 seconds and stops on a number. 6 or higher counts as correct; 5 or lower counts as wrong, retry. On a multiple-choice or sort question, a 20 also counts as the crit die's critical success (the big screen and phone both show 「大成功」 (critical success), the reward points get an extra half, and in the boss room the boss loses one extra heart); on a fill-in question a 20 only counts as correct. The host cannot judge during these 2 seconds. The use is spent the moment the button is pressed, and it is not given back if the phone disconnects or reloads during these 2 seconds. After a fill-in answer is submitted, this question can no longer be rolled, sneak-attacked or given the first character. When the narration talks about an attack, the weapon and move match the class (the mage uses spells, the warrior a sword or battle axe, the cleric holy light, the thief a dagger or throwing knives, the chef a kitchen knife or frying pan), in both scripted lines and AI narration; a team with no class hears lines that name no weapon. Design in [docs/designs/class-abilities.md](designs/class-abilities.md).

## Monsters into dishes

Every monster in the monster catalog comes with a dish (part, cooking method, dish name, review; see the [question bank guide](question-bank.en.md#default-question-bank)). When a correct answer defeats a monster, the big screen first reveals the answer as usual (a video plays as usual), with the monster just defeated on stage and 「🍲 {team that eats}」 at the top left. When the host presses 「🍲 上菜」 (serve, keyboard `1`), the video stops and the answer goes away; the monster jumps into the pot and stews, and after a puff of smoke the big screen shows the dish's pixel picture and the 「今晚的菜」 (tonight's dish) card. The narration says the dish's name and how much fullness the team ate, and the phones show 「🍲 今晚的菜：{dish}」 below the answer. 「下一題」 can be pressed about 2.6 seconds after serving. Each kind of monster has its own dish picture (a slime is a jelly, a ghost is shaved ice, the dragon is a skewer, and so on), colored like the catalog's original monster (a repeat appearance's new colour does not carry over). When the boss is defeated, the end screen first cooks the boss in the middle and serves tonight's main course; only about 4.6 seconds later does it start calling the places. Until then the phones show 「冒險結束！看大螢幕 📺」 (the adventure is over! watch the big screen). The reward points on screen are called 「飽足感」 (fullness), and the epilogue is a big feast that says what each team ate. Design in [docs/designs/monster-cuisine.md](designs/monster-cuisine.md).

## Event rooms

When a correct answer walks a team into its next room, there is a 25% chance that room is an event room: it holds no monster, and the team answers no question and gets no progress points. There is no draw when the team walks into the boss room, into the last room, or out of an event room. On the team's turn, the event itself stands in the big screen's arch, and the question panel shows 「❓ 事件房」 (event room) and the event's line. The team's phone shows a single button. Pressing it is the team's whole turn: the big screen acts out the outcome, the host presses 「下一題」 (next question) to pass the turn, and the team walks into its next room and faces that room's monster on its next turn. The other phones read 「{team}走進了事件房……看大螢幕 📺」 (the team walked into an event room… watch the big screen). After 15 seconds without a press, the host page shows 「替{team}按」 (press for the team). The six events are equally likely:

- Chest, 「🗝️ 打開寶箱」 (open the chest): a delicious dish, +20 fullness, or a terrible dish, −10 (never below 0). Both chests look the same until opened.
- Spring, 「🥤 喝一口」 (take a sip): one more class ability use; a chef or a team with no class gets +10 fullness instead.
- Skull idol, 「✋ 摸一下」 (touch it): a curse, the next correct answer pays half its base reward.
- Fairy, 「💬 跟精靈說話」 (talk to the fairy): a blessing, the next correct answer pays an extra half of its base reward.
- Fortune teller, 「🔮 請她算一卦」 (ask for a reading): a lot, one third each: 大吉 (great luck) +25, 中吉 (good luck) = the spring, 凶 (bad luck) = the curse.

The curse and the blessing last until the team's next correct answer (in a room or the boss room); a wrong answer does not use them up, and a new one replaces the old one. The big screen and the phone show 「💀 詛咒：下次答對減半」 (curse: next correct answer halved) or 「✨ 祝福：下次答對多一半」 (blessing: next correct answer +half) over the team's question. The curse does not stack with a persuasion check's natural 1 (the base is halved at most once); the blessing stacks with a critical success; the boss's 4d20 is never changed. The chef's 15% applies only to fullness an event gives, not to what it takes. An event turn takes no class abilities. Once any team is in the boss room, opening an event walks the team into the boss room, as a correct answer would; the event's effect still lands. A terrible dish at 0 fullness takes nothing. The host can send a team in an event room to the boss, which drops the event; 「指定下一題」 (choose the next question) deals when the team walks out, and a room the host already picked the next question for is never an event room. A room restart keeps the drawn event; nothing is redrawn. Design in [docs/designs/event-rooms.md](designs/event-rooms.md).
