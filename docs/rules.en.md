# Full rules

The README's "rules in one minute" section is the quick reference; this is the full version. For how the host runs the game, see the [hosting guide](hosting.en.md).

## Turns and question banks

**Initiative roll**: after the stone doors open at the start, each team presses 「🎲 擲骰」 (roll) on its phone to roll a d20. The big screen lines up every team's die, and the highest roll goes first. The room rerolls tied teams for them until the tie is broken. A team that has not rolled 15 seconds after the start (an offline one included) is rolled for by the room; an offline team gets the full 15 seconds too, and can still roll if its phone comes back in time. The host page has 「🎲 全部代擲」 (roll for everyone), which rolls for every team that has not rolled yet. After the order is set, the big screen holds it for about 4 seconds (2.5 seconds more for each reroll), then the first team starts answering, and the opening narration introduces the teams in this order.

**Global turns**: all teams answer in turn, in the order the initiative roll set, which stays the same all game. When a team's turn comes, the big screen shows the question, its difficulty and the monster the team faces. Questions are split into five banks by difficulty 1–5. Each room draws from the bank of its own difficulty. All teams share one deck, so two teams never get the same question at the same time (except in a custom bank with fewer questions than teams: two teams may start on one question, and as soon as a question whose answer nobody has seen comes free, one of them moves to it). The order of multiple-choice options is shuffled again at the start of every game. So are the items of a sort question, and they never come up in the correct order.

**Joining late**: a team that arrives after the game started joins the same way, with a team name and the room code (the password, team-name and 8-team rules are the same, and waiting teams count toward the 8). Its phone shows 「等主持人讓你們加入…」 (waiting for the host to let you in), and it can pick a class while it waits. When the host presses 「收這隊」 (let this team in), the team starts in the first room and goes last in the turn order, with no initiative roll; its reward points (飽足感) and progress points both start level with the team in last place at that moment (the lowest of each on the table), and the class it had picked at that moment is fixed. When the host presses 「不收」 (turn away), the phone shows 「主持人這場沒有收你們的隊伍」 (the host did not let your team into this game). Once any team is in the boss room nobody is let in: waiting phones show 「這場快結束了，等下一場」 (this game is almost over, wait for the next one), and they enter the lobby on their own when the host goes back to it. Design in [docs/designs/late-join.md](designs/late-join.md).

**Answer countdown**: in the lobby, above 「開始遊戲」, the host picks the time for each question (off, 30, 45, 60 or 90 seconds; 60 by default). It stays the same all game. The count starts as soon as the question is up. The big screen shows a large round clock to the right of the monster (when multiple-choice or sort tiles fill the wall, a small 「⏱ seconds」 pill under the tiles instead); the phone of the team on turn and the host page show 「⏱ seconds」. The last 5 seconds turn red and the big screen ticks once a second; at 0 it buzzes and shows 「⏰ 時間到」 (time's up). Time running out is only a reminder: nothing is judged wrong by itself, the host still judges. The count stops when the team locks its answer, sends a fill-in answer or rolls the warrior's die, or when the host judges. A persuasion check, a mage hint and a sneak attack do not stop it. A 「下輪再答」 (answer next round), a new question after fleeing, and the next team all start from the full time. A team choosing a door at a fork has no question yet, so there is no count (the fork has its own 15 seconds); after ⚔️ the count starts once the door has opened and the question is up. Nor is there a count while a mystery room's result is on show: the next team starts once the host presses 「下一題」. On a video or music question the count waits until the big screen has played the clip to its end (the host page shows 「⏱ 影片播完後開始倒數」). When every phone is offline the count is held and goes on once the team is back; a server restart keeps the time left too. Design: [docs/designs/answer-countdown.md](designs/answer-countdown.md).

## Judging

The host reads the question out (the question is on the big screen; the answer is only on the host's phone). The team types its answer on a phone (fill-in questions), and the host judges it with buttons. When hosting from a laptop, the host can also use the keyboard: `1` correct, `2` wrong and answer next round, `3` wrong and flee, same room, `4` wrong and flee, switch room (no `4` in the boss room); after the answer is revealed, `1` or Enter goes to the next question (for a monster with a dish, the first press is 「🍲 上菜」 (serve), the next one goes on). These keys do nothing while the pick list of 「指定下一題」 (pick next question) or `⚙ 更多` (more) is open.
- **Correct**: the team defeats this room's monster, gets its **reward points** (by default rooms 1–8 give 10/20/20/30/40/40/50/50), and moves on to the next room.
- **Wrong, answer next round**: the team stays in the same room; the next team goes first, and this team answers the same question again on its next turn.
- **Wrong, flee, same room** (「逃跑・同房」): the team stays in the same room, facing the same monster, but gets a new question from the bank one level lower, difficulty −1 (the reward points still follow this monster; nothing is deducted).
- **Wrong, flee, switch room** (「逃跑・換房」): like the same-room flee, difficulty −1 and a new question, and the progress does not change (the team stays at the same room number and gets no points), but it faces another monster: a different room monster of this game at the same difficulty (if there is none, a different room monster from the monster catalog, at random). When the team defeats it, the dish and the catalog count go to this new monster; the reward points still follow the room. Once the team leaves the room, the map's own monster is back. The boss room has no switch, only flee (a new question).
- **Change to correct**: when the host presses 「答錯」 (wrong) on a fill-in question by mistake, 「↩ 剛才的{隊名}改判答對」 (change {team}'s answer to correct) shows below the judge buttons (keyboard `0`). Pressing it counts that question as correct: score, reward points, dish and narration all go as for a correct answer, and a cleric's wait (with the answer-next-round or flee chosen after it) is taken back too. It works only until the game's next move: once the next team submits an answer, locks, rolls or uses a class ability, the host judges anything else, picks a next question or sends a team to the boss room, or the cleric prays, the button goes away. A phone disconnecting or reconnecting, or a team tapping an option (before locking), is not a move. The button cannot be pressed for half a second after it shows up, so a double press on 「答錯」 cannot land on it.

## Multiple-choice questions

**Multiple-choice questions** need no judging from the host: the team whose turn it is taps one option on its phone, then presses 「鎖定答案」 (lock answer). The result is revealed only after the host presses 「公布答案」 (reveal answer, keyboard `1`), so fill-in and multiple-choice questions have the same pace (design in [docs/designs/host-reveal.md](designs/host-reveal.md)). This team's phone shows the text of the question in a dark bar above the options (for an image or video question, its 題幹 (prompt), if it has one); it stays there through a wrong answer, an answer-next-round and the reveal of a correct answer. For image questions, the image sits right below 「輪到我們了！」 (our turn!). A video plays only on the big screen: in that spot the phone shows 「▶ 看大螢幕」 (watch the big screen), or 「🎵 聽大螢幕」 (listen to the big screen) for a music question. On a **fill-in question**, this team's phone also shows the question and an input box: type one answer (at most 40 characters) and press 「送出」 (submit). The host page shows 「隊伍答：…」 (team answered: …) below the question (「（還沒送出）」 (not submitted yet) before a submit), and the big screen shows 「⚔️ {隊名}：{答案}」 (team name: answer). Fill-in questions are still judged by the host, and the host can judge before anything is submitted (if a phone has trouble, the team can say the answer to the host). After a submit, it stays this team's turn until the host judges, even if the phone sleeps or disconnects. Other teams' phones do not show the question. After 「公布答案」 on a multiple-choice question, the big screen pauses 2 seconds, then reveals right or wrong. On a correct answer, a **crit die** (d20) is rolled next: the big screen dims, the die rolls for 2 seconds and stops on a number, the phone also shows the number rolled, and only then is the score counted. A 20 (critical success) gives an extra half of this question's reward points (the thief's sneak attack ×1.5 does not raise this half), and in the boss room the boss loses one extra heart. A 1 (critical failure) still counts as correct; only the narration and sound are different. If the phone disconnects during these 2 seconds of rolling, the team's correct answer still counts; the team is shown as offline only after the die stops. A correct fill-in answer does not roll the crit die. On a wrong answer, the big screen marks only the chosen wrong option in red; the correct answer is not revealed, and the team chooses on its phone 「下輪再答」 (answer next round, small print 「先換下一隊」, the next team first: the question stays, the next team goes, and this team answers it on its next turn; the wrong option turns grey and cannot be chosen again), 「逃跑・同房」 (flee, same room) or 「逃跑・換房」 (flee, switch room). After a correct answer, the answer stays on the big screen with one more line under 「正解：…」 (answer: …): 「✅ {隊名} 答對 +N 獎勵分」 ({team} correct +N reward points; 飽足感 (satiety) for a cooking bank; a correct fill-in answer shows it too), and the next question comes only when the host presses 「下一題」 (next question). If a team has not locked an answer when the answer countdown reaches 0 (15 seconds with the countdown 「關」 (off); also 15 seconds with no choice of answer-next-round or flee after a wrong answer), the host page shows 「{隊名}好像卡住了」 ({team} seems stuck) and 「替{隊名}選・下輪再答／逃跑・同房／逃跑・換房」 (choose for {team}: answer next round / flee, same room / flee, switch room), with keyboard `2`, `3` and `4` for these three buttons. On a multiple-choice question, `1` acts as 「公布答案」 only after the team has locked an answer. The boss room is an exception: after a wrong answer there is no answer-next-round-or-flee choice. The red option stays 2 seconds, then the boss automatically changes the question and the turn passes to the next team (the phone shows 「首領要換題了 ▶」 (the boss is changing the question)), so the next team never gets a question with options already removed. The host page's stuck row shows only 「下輪再答」, and keyboards `3` and `4` do nothing. Design in [docs/designs/phone-answering.md](designs/phone-answering.md) (for boss-room rules see 「首領房規則（v0.15）」 (boss room rules, v0.15)); for the question on the phone see [docs/designs/phone-question-text.md](designs/phone-question-text.md).

## Sort questions

A **sort question** gives four items and a rule to sort them by (for example 「依年齡由大到小排」 (oldest to youngest)); the rule is written in the question. The team whose turn it is holds the ☰ on the right of an item on its phone and drags it up or down to put the items in order (only the ☰ drags; anywhere else the page scrolls as usual; with a keyboard, focus the ☰ and press the up or down arrow), then presses 「鎖定答案」 (lock answer); above the items the phone reads 「按住 ☰ 拖曳排順序」 (hold ☰ and drag to order). From there it works like a multiple-choice question: the host presses 「公布答案」 (reveal answer), and the big screen pauses 2 seconds before the reveal. Only all four places right counts as correct; there are no partial points. A correct answer rolls the crit die, and the big screen and phone show the correct order (✓ on every place). On a wrong answer, the big screen and phone mark only whether each place in the team's order is right (✓, green) or wrong (✕, red); the correct order is not revealed, and the team chooses answer next round or flee. On its next try, the phone keeps the last order, so the team only fixes the wrong places; the same order cannot be locked twice. Answer next round, flee, the boss room's question change, the host's stuck row and the warrior's, thief's and cleric's abilities all work as for multiple choice; on a sort question the mage shows 「🔮 第 1 個：…」 (first item: …) (see [Classes](#classes)). The host page shows the correct order below the question (「答案（只有你看得到）」 (answer, only you can see it)), and the host cannot press 「答對」 (correct).

## Persuasion check

The input box of a fill-in question starts locked; the team whose turn it is must first press 「🎲 擲骰說服」 (roll to persuade) on its phone. The room rolls 1d20 plus the class bonus (mage +4, thief +3, cleric +2, chef +1, no class +1, warrior +0) against the monster's DC: difficulty 1–5 are 8/10/12/14/16 in order, and the boss is 16. The big screen shows 「🗣️ 說服 DC {DC}（+{加值}）」 (persuade DC {DC} (+{bonus})); the roll dims the screen and the die rolls for 2 seconds, as before. On a pass, both the phone and the big screen show 「🔮 第一個字：…」 (first character: …) (there is no hint when the answer is only one letter or digit, and the check does not give it again when the mage has already given the first character on this question). On a fail, the team just answers. A 20 is a persuasion success: no answer is needed and it counts as correct. A 1 is a persuasion failure: a correct answer on this question gets only half the base reward points (the thief's sneak attack still gives ×1.5, and a warrior's correct roll is halved too). Each team rolls only once per question: a retry does not roll again; fleeing to a new question rolls again. Until the check is rolled, the room accepts no typed answer, and the warrior cannot roll instead of answering; the host can still judge directly. If the phone disconnects during the 2 seconds of rolling, it is still this team's turn. If the room happens to restart during the 2 seconds of a 20, the persuasion success still counts when the team reconnects and it is its turn again (after any judging, reveal or phase transition in progress ends, and 4 seconds after the last sound cue); once the host judges that team, or the game goes back to the lobby or ends, it no longer counts. A pass and a fail each have a sound, and the narration has lines for a 20 and a 1. When a difficulty or the boss has no fill-in questions in the bank, there is no check there, and the bank editor warns about it.

## Progress points and the boss room

Each correct answer also adds **progress points** (equal to the current question's difficulty 1–5; fleeing makes the difficulty −1). A team that reaches **15** progress points enters the boss room; a team that passes the last room with fewer than 15 points stays in the last room and keeps fighting. As soon as one team is in the boss room, every other team goes straight to the boss room on its next correct answer. Only the host can see progress points (the host page shows 「進度/15」 (progress/15) for each team); the phones and the big screen show only reward points. The host can also send a stuck team straight to the boss room from 「⚙ 更多」. Next to each team on the host page is 「指定下一題」 (pick next question): the host can pick this team's next question from the bank of its next room's difficulty (questions whose answer has been revealed, that another team is answering, or that are already queued for another team cannot be picked). The next time this team moves to a question of that difficulty, it gets this question. When a team is in the boss room, the host can also pick the boss room's next question (when the boss has phases, only the current phase's questions are listed). The boss's health is shared, and teams take turns attacking.

**⚔️ 最後衝刺 (last sprint)**: the moment the first team enters the boss room (on a correct answer, or sent in by the host), the team with the lowest fullness outside the boss room gets a ✨ blessing (tied teams all get one). It works like the mystery room's blessing: the next correct answer pays an extra half of its base reward; it replaces a curse, and a team that already has a blessing gets nothing more. An mystery room's curse later still replaces it. It happens once per game, and not at all with one team, when every team starts in the boss room, or when no team is left outside. The big screen plays a purple 「⚔️ 魔王門開了！」 (the boss door is open!) banner once (with a fanfare when 🎵 music is on), and the owl says who walked in and who got the blessing (not when the host sent the team in). The blessed team's phone shows 「✨ 你們拿到祝福！」 (you got a blessing!) for 3 seconds and vibrates once, and a ✨ appears over that team's pawn on the map. A big screen or phone that connects later does not replay it. Design in [docs/designs/last-sprint.md](designs/last-sprint.md).

## Boss phases

A bank can split the boss fight into phases, each with its own name, rules, health bar and questions. When a phase's health runs out (or all its questions are used), the boss does not fall: the big screen reveals the answer as usual, and after the host presses 「下一題」 a 「還沒結束！」 (not over yet!) transition card appears with the new phase's name and rules; the boss changes color and its hearts fill back up one by one. Press 「下一題」 again to go on. Players do not know in advance how many phases there are; only the host page shows 「第 n/N 階段」 (phase n/N). Design in [docs/designs/boss-phases.md](designs/boss-phases.md).

## Buzzer phases

From phase 2 on, a bank can make a phase a **buzzer** phase (never phase 1). As soon as its transition card opens, every team still in an ordinary room enters the boss room, and turns stop: on each question every online team buzzes in at once. A music question opens for buzzing only after the host presses ▶ (play); any other question opens as soon as it shows. The phone has one big round button, 「搶答！」 (buzz!). The first team whose buzz reaches the room wins it: the big screen's music pauses and the screen shows 「某某隊 搶到了！」 (team X buzzed first!); the background music stays quiet until the host judges. That team says its answer out loud, and may also type it on the phone (optional), which shows only on the host page; the host presses 「答對」 (correct) or 「答錯」 (wrong). A correct answer is an ordinary boss hit (one heart off, the boss's reward points, the chef's extra 15% as usual, and the finishing blow still rolls 4 D20). A wrong answer costs no points, but that team cannot buzz on this question again; the music plays on (unless the host paused it by hand) and the other teams keep buzzing. When every online team has answered wrong, the same question opens again and everyone may buzz once more (the music waits for the host's ▶); only the host's 「沒人答對，換下一題」 (nobody got it, next question) brings the next question, and its answer is not revealed, so it may come up again later. When the bank gives questions a 「類別」 (category), the phone of the team that answered right shows a button for each category that still has questions in this phase; the team may pick or change until the host presses ▶ for the next song, the next question comes from that category, and the big screen shows 「下一題：某類別」 (next: category). With no pick, the questions go on in order. When the big screen cannot play the video, the host page shows a warning; the host plays the song from their own phone or speaker and presses 「現場放歌，開放搶答」 (play it live, open buzzing). If the team that buzzed drops offline, the buzz is released and the others may buzz again (it does not count as wrong). When the last team still able to buzz drops offline, it is as if everyone answered wrong: the same question opens again and the music waits for the host's ▶. Class abilities, persuasion checks and the cleric's prayer are off in a buzzer phase; a thief's sneak attack armed in the phase before is given back. The answer countdown does not run in a buzzer phase.

**Multi-field music questions**: a music question can have 2–3 answer fields (for example song title and singer). The phone of the team that buzzed shows one box per field; boxes may stay blank, but at least one needs text before it can be sent, and the team may also just say it out loud. Only the host sees what was typed; the other teams' phones and the big screen do not. The host presses ✓ or ✗ on each field, then 「送出判定」 (send the ruling): each right field takes 1 HP off the boss, and the reward is the share of fields right (one of two right earns half, rounded half up). One right field already counts as correct: the answer is revealed, the team picks the next song's category, and the finishing blow's 4 D20 are not cut. Every field wrong is an ordinary wrong buzz. On the reveal the big screen marks each field ✓ or ✗, and a partly right answer adds a line such as 「某某隊 對 1/2：打掉 1 格血、獎勵拿 50%」 (team X got 1/2: 1 HP off, 50% of the reward).

Design in [docs/designs/boss-buzzer.md](designs/boss-buzzer.md), [docs/designs/boss-music-buzzer.md](designs/boss-music-buzzer.md) and [docs/designs/multi-field.md](designs/multi-field.md).

## Boss scoring

Each hit on the boss gives **10** reward points; the **finishing blow** (the hit that brings the last phase's health to 0) adds 4 D20 (4–80 points). When the crit die rolls a 20, this hit gets an extra half (5 points by default) and the boss loses one extra heart; the 4 D20 of the finishing blow do not change. The big screen rolls those 4 dice first (they land one by one); the points are added, and the podium shows, only after they stop.

## Winning and playing again

The team with the highest total reward points wins. When a tie changes the top three, each tied team rolls a d20; the higher roll goes first, and teams still tied roll again until it is settled (ties from fourth place down are not rolled). Before the scores show, the narration hands out honor titles one at a time, one beat each, with a yellow card in the middle of the big screen showing the title, the team and the reason: 🗺️ 探險家 (explorer: most mystery rooms opened), ✨ 女神的寵兒 (the goddess's favorite: most mystery-room blessings), 💀 詛咒磁鐵 (curse magnet: most curses, the fortune teller's 「凶」 included), 🤢 腐肉品嚐家 (rotten-meat taster: most rotten dishes), 🏃 逃跑大師 (escape master: most flees, both kinds), 🧪 藥水成癮 (potion addict: most lucky potions drunk), 🍲 大胃王 (big eater: most dishes eaten), ⚡ 閃電突擊 (lightning raid: fewest turns to reach the boss room by its own correct answer) and 🎲 大成功王 (critical-success king: most natural 20s). Each title goes to the team with the highest value, tied teams share it, and a value of 0 gives no title; titles add no points and do not change the places. A team with no title gets a 🌟 title the AI makes up from how it played; when the AI is off or fails, it gets 🛡️ 「堅持到最後的冒險者」 (the adventurer who held out to the end). Design in [docs/designs/podium-titles.md](designs/podium-titles.md). After the titles, the narration calls the places from third to first, and the big screen fills the podium one place at a time; when dice settle a tie, the big screen rolls them first for everyone to see. The scores, the crown and the epilogue show only once first place is called, and only then do the phones show the places: every team's 「遊戲結束！」 (game over) card turns into a big place line, 「🥇 第 1 名！」 「🥈 第 2 名！」 「🥉 第 3 名！」 for the podium, then 「第 N 名・謝謝參與」 (Nth place, thanks for playing; tied teams below the podium share a place), with the team's reward points (飽足感 for a cooking bank) under it; the last question's answer card goes away. The big screen's podium still shows the top three only. Design in [docs/designs/podium-reveal.md](designs/podium-reveal.md). Defeating the boss only ends the game; it does not make that team the champion. The end screen names separately who landed the last blow. The host can also end early from 「⚙ 更多」 with 「結束冒險」 (end adventure) (then press 「確定結束？」 (really end?) once more): teams are ranked by their current reward points, with the same podium and epilogue, but there is no last blow, and the epilogue does not say the boss was defeated. Design in [docs/designs/scoring-and-score-pools.md](designs/scoring-and-score-pools.md). After the game ends, the host page has 「回到大廳，再玩一場」 (back to the lobby, play again) (only the host can see it): the phones, host page and big screen all go back to the lobby. Teams stay the same; team names, classes (which can be chosen again in the lobby), the join password and AI narration settings are kept; reward points and progress go back to zero. The next game first uses questions whose answers were not revealed in the last game, and only after those run out uses questions whose answers were revealed (boss phases too).

## Classes

In the lobby, each team can choose one class on a phone, or choose 「不選（普通冒險者）」 (none, ordinary adventurer). Any phone of the team can choose; the last choice before the start counts, and the class is fixed after the start (it can be chosen again when going back to the lobby to play again). The host page shows each team's class and its uses left (the chef is passive and shows no uses). The warrior and the mage have 4 uses per game; the cleric and the thief have 2. All five classes work. The mage removes 2 wrong options on a multiple-choice question; on a sort question, the mage lets the phone, host page and big screen see 「🔮 第 1 個：…」 (first item: the first item of the correct order, once per question); on a fill-in question, the mage lets the phone, host page and big screen see 「🔮 第一個字：…」 (the first letter or digit of the answer, skipping spaces and punctuation; once per question; it cannot be used, and no use is spent, when the answer is only one letter or digit or when this team's persuasion check has already given the first character). The cleric prays after a wrong answer to answer again this turn (on a fill-in question, after the host presses 「答錯」 (wrong, keyboard `2`), both the phone and the host page show four buttons: pray (on the phone, small print 「現在再答一次・剩 N 次」, answer again now, N left), answer next round, flee (same room), flee (switch room) (host page keyboard `2`, `3`, `4`, `5`; no switch in the boss room); whoever presses first counts). The thief presses 「偷襲」 (sneak attack) before answering: a correct answer on this question gives ×1.5 reward points (rounded half up, the boss's final 4d20 too) and ×2 boss damage; a wrong answer loses it. The chef's ability is passive, with nothing to press: every reward gain (飽足感) is 15% bigger, rounded half up and at least +1, taken on the whole gain (after the sneak attack, a critical success and the boss's 4d20); the big screen's dish card and the phone's correct line show 「（廚師 +N）」 (chef +N). When it is the warrior's turn, the phone shows 「🎲 擲骰代替答題」 (roll instead of answering) below 「鎖定答案」 (on a fill-in question it is below 「送出」, and the persuasion check must be rolled first): the room rolls 1d20 at once, the big screen dims, and the die rolls for 2 seconds and stops on a number. 9 or higher counts as correct; 8 or lower counts as wrong, answer next round. The narration says the number and whether it hit. On a multiple-choice or sort question, a 20 also counts as the crit die's critical success (the big screen and phone both show 「大成功」 (critical success), the reward points get an extra half, and in the boss room the boss loses one extra heart); on a fill-in question a 20 only counts as correct. The host cannot judge during these 2 seconds. The use is spent the moment the button is pressed, and it is not given back if the phone disconnects or reloads during these 2 seconds. After a fill-in answer is submitted, this question can no longer be rolled, sneak-attacked or given the first character. When the narration talks about an attack, the weapon and move match the class (the mage uses spells, the warrior a sword or battle axe, the cleric holy light, the thief a dagger or throwing knives, the chef a kitchen knife or frying pan), in both scripted lines and AI narration; a team with no class hears lines that name no weapon. Design in [docs/designs/class-abilities.md](designs/class-abilities.md).

## Monsters into dishes

Every monster in the monster catalog comes with a dish (part, cooking method, dish name, review; see the [question bank guide](question-bank.en.md#default-question-bank)). When a correct answer defeats a monster, the big screen first reveals the answer as usual (a video plays as usual), with the monster just defeated on stage and 「🍲 {team that eats}」 at the top left. When the host presses 「🍲 上菜」 (serve, keyboard `1`), the video stops and the answer goes away; the monster jumps into the pot and stews, and after a puff of smoke the big screen shows the dish's pixel picture and the 「今晚的菜」 (tonight's dish) card. The narration says the dish's name and how much fullness the team ate, and the phones show 「🍲 今晚的菜：{dish}」 below the answer. 「下一題」 can be pressed about 2.6 seconds after serving. Each kind of monster has its own dish picture (a slime is a jelly, a ghost is shaved ice, the dragon is a skewer, and so on), colored like the catalog's original monster (a repeat appearance's new colour does not carry over). When the boss is defeated, the end screen first cooks the boss in the middle and serves tonight's main course; only about 4.6 seconds later does it start calling the places. Until then the phones show 「冒險結束！看大螢幕 📺」 (the adventure is over! watch the big screen). The reward points on screen are called 「飽足感」 (fullness), and the epilogue is a big feast that says what each team ate. Design in [docs/designs/monster-cuisine.md](designs/monster-cuisine.md).

## Forks and mystery rooms

When a correct answer walks a team into its next room, there is a 25% chance that room is a fork. There is no draw when the team walks into the boss room, into the last room, out of a mystery room, or out of a room it took ⚔️ into, so two forks are always at least one room apart. On a draw, 🚪 shows at the top right of the team's name tag on the map and the team's row on the host page reads 「🚪 在叉路口」 (at a fork); the other teams play on as usual. The team's next turn opens with the choice, before any question. The big screen shows 「🔀 叉路！」 (a fork!) and the narration switches to a fork opening line (for example 「{team}走到了叉路口……左邊還是右邊？」, the team reaches a fork… left or right?) that says nothing about what is behind the doors; the middle archway is walled up and a doorway on each side holds a door, labelled only 「左門」 (left door) and 「右門」 (right door), with a 15-second round clock; the team's phone shows two big buttons, 「左門」 and 「右門」, which stay on screen. One door hides ⚔️ 怪物房 (monster room), the other ❓ 神秘房 (mystery room). The server picks which side is which at random at every fork; the big screen, the phones and the host page never see it, and the host does not know either. The other phones read 「{team}在叉路口猶豫中……看大螢幕 📺」 (the team is hesitating at a fork… watch the big screen); the host page reads 「{team}在叉路口，選左門或右門」 (the team is at a fork, choosing the left or right door) with the seconds left, and the host cannot choose for the team.

- ⚔️ monster room: the room holds its monster and question as usual; scoring and progress are the same as with no fork.
- ❓ mystery room: the press draws an event and applies its effect at once (see below). The event replaces the room's monster: the team gives up the room's reward points, dish and progress points for the event's effect (good or bad), walks straight through the room, and faces the next room's monster on its next turn. While the result is on the big screen, the next team's phone reads 「下一個是我們！」 (we are next!) in its banner; it turns into 「輪到我們了！」 (our turn!) once the host presses 「下一題」 and the question is up.
- No choice in 15 seconds: the team takes ⚔️, and the monster room's door is the one that opens. Going offline does not end it early, and a team back within the 15 seconds can still choose; a team that drops while choosing keeps the turn until it chooses or time runs out. A team already offline when its turn comes is skipped as usual, and the fork waits, unclocked, for its next turn. With the host's [manual pacing](hosting.en.md#manual-pacing) on there is no countdown: the doors wait for the team, or for the host's 「下一步」, which counts as time running out.

Once a door is chosen, the big screen plays a door opening of about 1.5 seconds: the chosen door swings open and its 「左門」 or 「右門」 label turns into the room behind it (「⚔️ 怪物房」 or 「❓ 神秘房」), on its phone button too; the other door dims and is never revealed. On a timeout 「⏳ 時間到」 (time's up) shows first, then the monster room's door opens. On ⚔️, the question comes up after the opening, the narration reads a scripted line that introduces the monster behind the door (no AI wait), and the answer countdown starts only once the question is up; the fork's 15 seconds do not count. On ❓, the event is drawn and its effect worked out at the press, but the big screen, the phones and the scores show it only after the opening; then the big screen acts out the event and the host presses 「下一題」 (next question) to pass the turn. The 1.5-second opening counts as a pause: the host's 「下一題」 and the team's actions are refused. A team at a fork cannot answer, use a class ability or drink a potion. The six events are equally likely:

- Chest: a delicious dish, +20 fullness, or a terrible dish, −10 (never below 0). Both chests look the same until opened.
- Spring: one more class ability use; a chef or a team with no class gets +10 fullness instead.
- Skull idol: a curse, the next correct answer pays half its base reward.
- Fairy: a blessing, the next correct answer pays an extra half of its base reward.
- Fortune teller: a lot, one third each: 大吉 (great luck) +25, 中吉 (good luck) = the spring, 凶 (bad luck) = the curse.

The curse and the blessing last until the team's next correct answer (in a room or the boss room); a wrong answer does not use them up, and a new one replaces the old one. The big screen and the phone show 「💀 詛咒：下次答對減半」 (curse: next correct answer halved) or 「✨ 祝福：下次答對多一半」 (blessing: next correct answer +half) over the team's question. The curse does not stack with a persuasion check's natural 1 (the base is halved at most once); the blessing stacks with a critical success; the boss's 4d20 is never changed. The chef's 15% applies only to fullness an event gives, not to what it takes. Once any team is in the boss room, choosing ❓ walks the team into the boss room, as a correct answer would; the event's effect still lands. A terrible dish at 0 fullness takes nothing. The host can send a team at a fork to the boss, which drops the fork; 「指定下一題」 (choose the next question) deals in the next room as usual (after ⚔️, the room after this monster's; after ❓, the room the team walks into), and a room the host already picked the next question for is never a fork. A room restart keeps the team at its fork with its deadline (the clock does not pause); if the deadline has passed by then, the team takes ⚔️. A restart during the opening keeps a ❓ effect but does not act it out again. The game log records, for each fork, the side opened, the room behind it, and whether time ran out. Design in [docs/designs/branch-fork.md](designs/branch-fork.md) and [docs/designs/event-rooms.md](designs/event-rooms.md).

## Lucky potion

Every team row on the host page has 「🧪 給藥水」 (give a potion). The host can press it at any time, for any team, on turn or not (for a funny answer or loud cheering, say). A team holds at most one: while it has one, the button is greyed out as 「🧪 已有」 (has one). On a give, the owl on the big screen reads one more line, 「🧪 {team}拿到一瓶幸運藥水！」 (got a lucky potion), a 🧪 appears on the corner of the team's name tag on the map, and the team card on its phone reads 「🧪 有一瓶幸運藥水」 (holds a lucky potion). On its own turn, the phone shows 「🧪 喝下幸運藥水」 (drink the lucky potion, small print 「下一顆骰子擲兩次取高」: the next die rolls twice, keep the higher) above 「鎖定答案」 (lock answer), or above 「🎲 擲骰說服」 (roll to persuade) on a fill-in question. One tap drinks it, with no undo; the chip over the question on the big screen changes from 「🧪 有幸運藥水」 (has a lucky potion) to 「🧪 已喝下！」 (drunk!). Once drunk, the next crit die or persuasion check on this question rolls twice and keeps the higher: two dice tumble together on the big screen, the higher one lights up, and the result bar reads 「取高：{n}」 (keeps {n}). That roll uses the potion up. The warrior's answer die and the boss's 4d20 never use it. When the turn ends without a roll (a wrong answer, a flee, or answering with the warrior's die), the team keeps the potion, and its phone reads 「🧪 藥水還在：這題沒擲到骰」 (potion kept: no die rolled); it drinks again on a later turn to use it. A team cannot drink in a buzzer phase, at a fork, or once its answer is locked or its check is rolled. Design in [docs/designs/lucky-potion.md](designs/lucky-potion.md).

## Audience reactions

A team whose turn it is not has a row of four reaction buttons above the narration box on its phone: 👏 鼓掌 (clap), 😱 好險 (close one), 😂 笑死 (LOL) and 👎 噓～ (boo). A tap makes that emoji, with the team's name, rise on the big screen from behind the options up the side walls, gone in about 2 seconds. It is drawn behind the question, the options, the countdown and the narration box, so it never covers them. There are only these four, and no typing. Each team can send one every 2 seconds (the four buttons go grey for 2 seconds after a tap; a tap that arrives up to 0.3 seconds early over a slow network still counts), and the whole room at most 7 in about every 2 seconds (with 8 teams, the other 7 can all react at once); any more are dropped. There are no reaction buttons on a team's own turn, in a buzzer phase (everyone is answering), during the how-to-play intro, while rolling initiative or while the phone is offline, and the server drops reactions at those times too. Reactions score nothing, are not saved, and never change whose turn it is. When at least two different waiting teams tap during one team's turn, at least 3 times in all with at least 2 of the same reaction, the next judged narration adds a line about the team that tapped it most, for example 「藍莓在後面噓聲四起……」 (Blueberry boos from the back); it never names the team up next, which is called right after. Two judgments in a row never both get one, nor does the game's last line, and a line the host queued takes its place. Design in [docs/designs/audience-reactions.md](designs/audience-reactions.md).
