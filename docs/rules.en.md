# Full rules

The README's "rules in one minute" section is the quick reference; this is the full version. For how the host runs the game, see the [hosting guide](hosting.en.md).

## Turns and question banks

**Global turns**: all teams answer in turn, in a fixed order (A → B → C → …). When a team's turn comes, the big screen shows the question, its difficulty and the monster the team faces. Questions are split into five banks by difficulty 1–5. Each room draws from the bank of its own difficulty. All teams share one deck, so two teams never get the same question at the same time. The order of multiple-choice options is shuffled again at the start of every game.

## Judging

The host reads the question out (the question is on the big screen; the answer is only on the host's phone). The team types its answer on a phone (fill-in questions), and the host judges it with buttons. When hosting from a laptop, the host can also use the keyboard: `1` correct, `2` wrong and retry, `3` wrong and flee; after the answer is revealed, `1` or Enter goes to the next question. These keys do nothing while the pick list of 「指定下一題」 (pick next question) or `⚙ 更多` (more) is open.
- **Correct**: the team defeats this room's monster, gets its **reward points** (by default rooms 1–8 give 10/20/20/30/40/40/50/50), and moves on to the next room.
- **Wrong, retry**: the team stays in the same room and answers the same question again.
- **Wrong, flee**: the team stays in the same room, facing the same monster, but gets a new question from the bank one level lower, difficulty −1 (the reward points still follow this monster; nothing is deducted).

## Multiple-choice questions

**Multiple-choice questions** need no judging from the host: the team whose turn it is taps one option on its phone, then presses 「鎖定答案」 (lock answer). The result is revealed only after the host presses 「公布答案」 (reveal answer, keyboard `1`), so fill-in and multiple-choice questions have the same pace (design in [docs/designs/host-reveal.md](designs/host-reveal.md)). This team's phone shows the text of the question in a dark bar above the options (for an image or video question, its 題幹 (prompt), if it has one); it stays there through a wrong answer, a retry and the reveal of a correct answer. For image questions, the image sits right below 「輪到我們了！」 (our turn!). A video plays only on the big screen: in that spot the phone shows 「▶ 看大螢幕」 (watch the big screen), or 「🎵 聽大螢幕」 (listen to the big screen) for a music question. On a **fill-in question**, this team's phone also shows the question and an input box: type one answer (at most 40 characters) and press 「送出」 (submit). The host page shows 「隊伍答：…」 (team answered: …) below the question (「（還沒送出）」 (not submitted yet) before a submit), and the big screen shows 「⚔️ {隊名}：{答案}」 (team name: answer). Fill-in questions are still judged by the host, and the host can judge before anything is submitted (if a phone has trouble, the team can say the answer to the host). After a submit, it stays this team's turn until the host judges, even if the phone sleeps or disconnects. Other teams' phones do not show the question. After 「公布答案」 on a multiple-choice question, the big screen pauses 2 seconds, then reveals right or wrong. On a correct answer, a **crit die** (d20) is rolled next: the big screen dims, the die rolls for 2 seconds and stops on a number, the phone also shows the number rolled, and only then is the score counted. A 20 (critical success) gives an extra half of this question's reward points (the thief's sneak attack ×2 does not double this half), and in the boss room the boss loses one extra heart. A 1 (critical failure) still counts as correct; only the narration and sound are different. If the phone disconnects during these 2 seconds of rolling, the team's correct answer still counts; the team is shown as offline only after the die stops. A correct fill-in answer does not roll the crit die. On a wrong answer, the big screen marks only the chosen wrong option in red; the correct answer is not revealed, and the team chooses 「重答」 (retry) on its phone (the wrong option turns grey and cannot be chosen again) or 「逃跑」 (flee). After a correct answer, the answer stays on the big screen, and the next question comes only when the host presses 「下一題」 (next question). If a team has not locked an answer in 15 seconds (or, after a wrong answer, has not chosen retry or flee in 15 seconds), the host page shows 「替{隊名}選・重答／逃跑」 (choose for {team}: retry / flee), with keyboard `2` and `3` for these two buttons. On a multiple-choice question, `1` acts as 「公布答案」 only after the team has locked an answer. The boss room is an exception: after a wrong answer there is no retry-or-flee choice. The red option stays 2 seconds, then the boss automatically changes the question and the turn passes to the next team (the phone shows 「首領要換題了 ▶」 (the boss is changing the question)), so the next team never gets a question with options already removed. The host page's stuck row shows only 「重答」, and keyboard `3` does nothing. Design in [docs/designs/phone-answering.md](designs/phone-answering.md) (for boss-room rules see 「首領房規則（v0.15）」 (boss room rules, v0.15)); for the question on the phone see [docs/designs/phone-question-text.md](designs/phone-question-text.md).

## Persuasion check

The input box of a fill-in question starts locked; the team whose turn it is must first press 「🎲 擲骰說服」 (roll to persuade) on its phone. The room rolls 1d20 plus the class bonus (thief +4, cleric +3, mage +2, no class +1, warrior +0) against the monster's DC: difficulty 1–5 are 8/10/12/14/16 in order, and the boss is 16. The big screen shows 「🗣️ 說服 DC {DC}（+{加值}）」 (persuade DC {DC} (+{bonus})); the roll dims the screen and the die rolls for 2 seconds, as before. On a pass, both the phone and the big screen show 「🔮 第一個字：…」 (first character: …) (there is no hint when the answer is only one letter or digit, and the check does not give it again when the mage has already given the first character on this question). On a fail, the team just answers. A 20 is a persuasion success: no answer is needed and it counts as correct. A 1 is a persuasion failure: a correct answer on this question gets only half the base reward points (the thief's sneak attack still gives ×2, and a warrior's correct roll is halved too). Each team rolls only once per question: a retry does not roll again; fleeing to a new question rolls again. Until the check is rolled, the room accepts no typed answer, and the warrior cannot roll instead of answering; the host can still judge directly. If the phone disconnects during the 2 seconds of rolling, it is still this team's turn. If the room happens to restart during the 2 seconds of a 20, the persuasion success still counts when the team reconnects and it is its turn again (after any judging, reveal or phase transition in progress ends). A pass and a fail each have a sound, and the narration has lines for a 20 and a 1. When a difficulty or the boss has no fill-in questions in the bank, there is no check there, and the bank editor warns about it.

## Progress points and the boss room

Each correct answer also adds **progress points** (equal to the current question's difficulty 1–5; fleeing makes the difficulty −1). A team that reaches **15** progress points enters the boss room; a team that passes the last room with fewer than 15 points stays in the last room and keeps fighting. As soon as one team is in the boss room, every other team goes straight to the boss room on its next correct answer. Only the host can see progress points (the host page shows 「進度/15」 (progress/15) for each team); the phones and the big screen show only reward points. The host can also send a stuck team straight to the boss room from 「⚙ 更多」. Next to each team on the host page is 「指定下一題」 (pick next question): the host can pick this team's next question from the bank of its next room's difficulty (questions whose answer has been revealed, that another team is answering, or that are already queued for another team cannot be picked). The next time this team moves to a question of that difficulty, it gets this question. When a team is in the boss room, the host can also pick the boss room's next question (when the boss has phases, only the current phase's questions are listed). The boss's health is shared, and teams take turns attacking.

## Boss phases

A bank can split the boss fight into phases, each with its own name, rules, health bar and questions. When a phase's health runs out (or all its questions are used), the boss does not fall: the big screen reveals the answer as usual, and after the host presses 「下一題」 a 「還沒結束！」 (not over yet!) transition card appears with the new phase's name and rules; the boss changes color and its hearts fill back up one by one. Press 「下一題」 again to go on. Players do not know in advance how many phases there are; only the host page shows 「第 n/N 階段」 (phase n/N). Design in [docs/designs/boss-phases.md](designs/boss-phases.md).

## Boss scoring

Each hit on the boss gives **10** reward points; the **finishing blow** (the hit that brings the last phase's health to 0) adds 4 D20 (4–80 points). When the crit die rolls a 20, this hit gets an extra half (5 points by default) and the boss loses one extra heart; the 4 D20 of the finishing blow do not change. The big screen rolls those 4 dice first (they land one by one); the points are added, and the podium shows, only after they stop.

## Winning and playing again

The team with the highest total reward points wins; tied teams share first place. Defeating the boss only ends the game; it does not make that team the champion. The end screen names separately who landed the last blow. The host can also end early from 「⚙ 更多」 with 「結束冒險」 (end adventure) (then press 「確定結束？」 (really end?) once more): teams are ranked by their current reward points, with the same podium and epilogue, but there is no last blow, and the epilogue does not say the boss was defeated. Design in [docs/designs/scoring-and-score-pools.md](designs/scoring-and-score-pools.md). After the game ends, the host page has 「回到大廳，再玩一場」 (back to the lobby, play again) (only the host can see it): the phones, host page and big screen all go back to the lobby. Teams stay the same; team names, classes (which can be chosen again in the lobby), the join password, AI narration settings and director notes are kept; reward points and progress go back to zero. The next game first uses questions whose answers were not revealed in the last game, and only after those run out uses questions whose answers were revealed (boss phases too).

## Classes

In the lobby, each team can choose one class on a phone, or choose 「不選（普通冒險者）」 (none, ordinary adventurer). Any phone of the team can choose; the last choice before the start counts, and the class is fixed after the start (it can be chosen again when going back to the lobby to play again). The host page shows each team's class and its uses left. All four classes work. The mage removes 2 wrong options on a multiple-choice question; on a fill-in question, the mage lets the phone, host page and big screen see 「🔮 第一個字：…」 (the first letter or digit of the answer, skipping spaces and punctuation; once per question; it cannot be used, and no use is spent, when the answer is only one letter or digit or when this team's persuasion check has already given the first character). The cleric prays after a wrong answer to answer again this turn (on a fill-in question, after the host presses 「答錯」 (wrong, keyboard `2`), both the phone and the host page show three buttons: pray, retry, flee (host page keyboard `2`, `3`, `4`); whoever presses first counts). The thief presses 「偷襲」 (sneak attack) before answering: a correct answer on this question gives ×2 reward points and ×2 boss damage; a wrong answer loses it. When it is the warrior's turn, the phone shows 「🎲 擲骰代替答題」 (roll instead of answering) below 「鎖定答案」 (on a fill-in question it is below 「送出」, and the persuasion check must be rolled first), 2 times per game: the room rolls 1d20 at once, the big screen dims, and the die rolls for 2 seconds and stops on a number. 12 or higher counts as correct; 11 or lower counts as wrong, retry. On a multiple-choice question, a 20 also counts as the crit die's critical success (the big screen and phone both show 「大成功」 (critical success), the reward points get an extra half, and in the boss room the boss loses one extra heart); on a fill-in question a 20 only counts as correct. The host cannot judge during these 2 seconds. The use is spent the moment the button is pressed, and it is not given back if the phone disconnects or reloads during these 2 seconds. After a fill-in answer is submitted, this question can no longer be rolled, sneak-attacked or given the first character. When the narration talks about an attack, the weapon and move match the class (the mage uses spells, the warrior a sword or battle axe, the cleric holy light, the thief a dagger or throwing knives), in both scripted lines and AI narration; a team with no class hears lines that name no weapon. Design in [docs/designs/class-abilities.md](designs/class-abilities.md).

## Monsters into dishes

A monster in the bank can come with a dish (part, cooking method, dish name, review). When a correct answer defeats a monster that has a dish, the big screen serves 「今晚的菜」 (tonight's dish) below the correct answer, and the narration mentions the dish too; defeating the boss gives tonight's main course. As soon as any monster in the bank has a dish, the reward points on screen are renamed 「飽足感」 (fullness), and the epilogue is a big feast that says what each team ate. Every monster in the default bank has a dish. Design in [docs/designs/monster-cuisine.md](designs/monster-cuisine.md).
