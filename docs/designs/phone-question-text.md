# Design: The answering team's phone shows the question

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-10-28
Branch: phone-question-text
Repo: chihgeorge/critical-answer
Status: APPROVED
Mode: Builder

## Problem Statement

On its own multiple-choice turn, the answering team looks down at its phone to pick an option. The phone shows only the four options, not the question, so the team forgets the question and has to look back up at the TV. In the user's words: "看選項時忘了題目" (they forget the question while reading the options).

Today `/play` already receives the question (`PublicQuestion`, which has no answer). It draws image and YouTube content, but never the text of a text question.

## What Makes This Cool

The team can argue over the options with the question right there in their hands, and the TV still holds the room's attention. The phone becomes a reminder card for the people who are answering, not a second screen for everyone.

## Constraints

- Answers never reach a phone. Only server code reads `BANK` (CLAUDE.md), and `npm run check:bundle` must pass.
- No server or protocol change: the phone already has `game.question`.
- The design of phone answering (`docs/designs/phone-answering.md`, D2) put the question only on the TV, so that everyone looks up. This design keeps that for every team except the one answering.

## Premises

1. The pain belongs to the answering team: when they look down at the options, they need the question there too.
2. The TV stays the main screen. `/screen` keeps showing the question large, and the phone copy is only a reminder.
3. No server change. The phone already receives the question without its answer, so only the phone screen changes.
4. On an iPhone 12 (390×844) or an iPhone Air and larger, the question, the four options and the 鎖定答案 button fit on one screen with no scrolling. This replaces D2's iPhone SE (375×667) target for this screen; the user chose it.

## Approaches Considered

- **A: only the answering team sees it, above the options in the same panel.** Chosen.
- **B: every phone shows the question (Kahoot's "Show questions & answers" toggle).** Rejected: every team then has a reason to look down, and the TV loses the room.
- **C: A, plus long questions collapse to 3 lines with tap to expand.** Rejected for now: the longest text question in the default bank is 34 characters (r2-q2, r3-q1), about 2 lines at 20px in a 340px panel, and C adds an interaction state.

## Recommended Approach

Approach A.

- On the team's own multiple-choice turn, `MyChoicePanel` shows a **text** question at the top of its `Panel tone="turn"`, as the panel's first child, above the 答錯了 header and the option buttons. It is a heading element, and the option group points at it with `aria-labelledby`, so a screen reader reads the question before the options.
- The question stays at the top in every sub-state of the panel: picking, lock in flight (鎖定中…), locked (beat), wrong lock with 重答 / 逃跑, and the boss-miss beat (答錯了！ then 首領要換題了 ▶).
- Normal room, after 重答: the same question comes back on the team's next turn, still at the top, with the eliminated options grey.
- Boss room, after a wrong lock: the panel keeps the old question through the beat. It shows the new question once the room deals it.
- Image and YouTube questions stay out of the turn panel. On the answering team's own turn, the existing media panel sits right under the turn panel (design review D5). The media panel has one fixed slot right after the turn panel; the team, branch and monster cards render above it on waiting turns and below it on the team's own turn (eng review R1). The media node never moves, so it is never remounted and a video does not restart.
- Other teams' phones stay as they are today, media panel included.
- When the team's own lock is right (the D14 reveal panel: green tile and 答對了！＋N 分), the same question strip stays above the tiles (design review D4). During the reveal `game.question` is already the next question, and `AnswerReveal` carries no question text, so the phone keeps the last question it showed and matches it by `reveal.questionId` (design review D6). A phone that reconnects during the reveal has no stored question and shows the reveal panel without the strip, as today.
- The sub-session's unreviewed patch (every phone shows the question) is kept only as a local-only file in the session scratchpad, outside the repo. It is not the chosen approach, it is not applied, and it never goes into the PR.

## Design Review Decisions (/plan-design-review, 2026-10-29)

Mockups were rendered from the project's real compiled CSS and Cubic 11 font at 390×844 (the gstack designer had no OpenAI key). They are local-only, in the session scratchpad.

- **D2: question strip style C.** A full-width strip at the top of the turn panel: `--k` background, `--cream` text, 20px Cubic 11, line-height 1.35, padding 10px 12px, flush with the panel's top and side edges (negative margin over the panel's 10px padding). Contrast about 14:1. It shares no look with the option tiles, so nobody reads it as a fifth option, and it stays apart from the 答錯了 header. It is an `h2`; the options sit in a `role="group"` container whose `aria-labelledby` points at it.
- **D4: the strip stays on the team's own correct-lock reveal panel.**
- **D5: media under the turn panel on the team's own turn**, with no remount. (Eng review R1 replaced the CSS `order` mechanism with a fixed media slot.)
- **D6: the reveal strip uses the phone's last shown question**, matched by `reveal.questionId`.

Height budget, measured on the mockups (bottom of the turn panel from the top of the page, longest question):

| State | Bottom of turn panel |
|-------|----------------------|
| Picking, short question | 485px |
| Picking, 34-character question | 512px |
| Wrong lock with 重答 / 逃跑 | 569px |

Safari on an iPhone 12 shows about 660px with its address bar expanded, so every state fits. A boss turn adds the phase-rule panel (about 45px), and a failed lock adds one 20px line; the worst case still ends near 590px.

### NOT in scope
- Waiting teams' phones: no question text, media unchanged (premise 2, D5).
- Collapsing long questions (approach C): the longest default question is 2 lines.
- Shrinking media on the answering phone: with D5, in the wrong-lock state the media panel is partly below the fold. Acceptable for this round.

### What already exists
`Panel tone="turn"`, `McOption`, `PixelButton`, the `--k` / `--cream` tokens, the Cubic 11 font, `QuestionMedia`, and the page's flex column on `/play`.

## Success Criteria

- On its own multiple-choice turn, the answering team sees a text question above the options in every panel sub-state, in normal rooms and the boss room. After a boss miss it sees the new question.
- Before the reveal, no phone except the answering team's shows a text question.
- On its own host-judged text turn, the answering team sees the same question strip under the ribbon (eng review R2).
- On an iPhone 12 viewport (390×844), the longest text question in the default bank, the four options and 鎖定答案 fit without scrolling. The same holds in the wrong-lock state with 重答 / 逃跑.
- On its own correct lock, the answering team sees the question strip above the green tile until the reveal closes (unless the phone reconnected during the reveal).
- On its own image or YouTube turn, the media panel sits directly under the turn panel; a playing video does not restart when the turn starts or ends.
- No answer or `correctIndex` reaches any phone before the reveal. `check:bundle` passes.
- Unit and e2e tests cover these points, including the heading and its `aria-labelledby` link.
