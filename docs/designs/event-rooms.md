## Problem Statement
Every room in the dungeon is a quiz monster. The owner asked on 2026-10-30 for a surprise now and then: a room that holds an event instead, good or bad, in the spirit of the chance squares in board games. It must be ready, and fun, for the event on 2026-11-21.

## What Makes This Cool
- **The event is acted out on stage, not shown as a card.** The thing itself stands in the arch where the monster would: a locked treasure chest, a glowing spring, a skull idol, a fairy, a fortune teller with her crystal ball. The team presses one action on its phone, and the stage plays out what happens.
- **Satiety is food.** A chest that pays out holds a delicious dish (+20). A chest that costs holds a terrible dish (−10). Both chests look the same until opened.
- **Bad events have bait.** Players walk into them on their own: they touch the idol, they open the chest.

## Decisions
- **D1 Event room replaces the monster.** The team that draws one skips that room's monster (no dish, no reward, no progress points). Rejected: a card shown on room entry with the monster still there.
- **D2 Drawn per team, on room entry.** When a correct answer walks a team into its next room, the room rolls; the same room can be a monster for one team and an event for another. The map is unchanged. Rejected: fixed event rooms marked ❓ on the map (more map work).
- **D3 The event is the team's whole turn.** The team's phone shows one action button. Pressing it plays the event on the TV and applies the effect. The host presses 下一題, and the next team plays. The event team walks into its next room and faces that room's monster on its next turn.
- **D4, D7, D8, D9 The six events** (uniform draw, 1/6 each):

  | Event | On stage | Phone button | Effect |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 美味料理 | locked chest | 🗝️ 打開寶箱 | a dish from the monster catalog, +20 satiety |
  | 黑暗料理 | the same locked chest | 🗝️ 打開寶箱 | a purple-smoking dish, −10 satiety (never below 0) |
  | 回復泉水 | glowing spring | 🥤 喝一口 | class ability +1 use; a chef or a team with no class gets +10 satiety instead |
  | 詛咒 | skull idol | ✋ 摸一下 | the team's next correct answer pays half its base reward |
  | 祝福 | fairy | 💬 跟精靈說話 | the team's next correct answer pays an extra half of its base reward |
  | 占卜屋 | fortune teller | 🔮 請她算一卦 | a lot, 1/3 each: 大吉 +25 satiety · 中吉 = the spring's effect · 凶 = the curse |

  The merchant was dropped: buying with satiety made no sense to the owner, and a merchant who gives things away is not a merchant. The fortune teller replaced it.
- **D5 Chance 25%** (`EVENT_CHANCE`). It is rolled only when a correct answer moves a team to a new room. It is not rolled when the team walks into the boss room, into the last room, or out of an event room. So two event rooms never come in a row, and a team stuck in the last room keeps fighting. Expect about 8 events in a 6-team game. Tune it later from the game logs.
- **D6 TV** (mockup `img/event-rooms-screen.png`): the event sprite stands in the arch, with a nameplate. The question panel carries a ❓ 事件房 chip and the scene line, for example 「房間裡沒有怪物，只有一個寶箱……」 and 「{隊名}：按手機上的「打開寶箱」」. After the press, the stage shows the outcome sprite (an open chest with the dish, the smoke, the lot). The panel shows the outcome title in gold and the effect line. The panel stays up until 下一題, like an answer reveal.
- **D10 Phone** (mockup `img/event-rooms-phone.png`): the turn team sees the scene line and one big action button; after the press it sees the outcome and 「看大螢幕 📺」. Other phones read 「{隊名}走進了事件房……看大螢幕 📺」. The host page shows the event and a 「替{隊名}按」 button after 15 seconds without a press, like the stuck row.
- **D11 Narration, scripted and AI.** (1) The "next" line before an event turn sets the scene without spoiling it: 「{隊名}推開門，房間正中央擺著一個寶箱……」. (2) After the press, a new narration event `event-room` reads the outcome with its numbers, then the usual next line. The scripted bank has 2–3 lines per event and outcome. The AI gets the event, outcome, numbers and next turn on the same pipeline, and falls back to the scripted lines as today.
- **D12 Rules where events meet the rest:**
  - No events in the boss room. A team in an event room can still be sent to the boss by the host; the event is dropped.
  - Once any team is in the boss room, opening an event walks the team into the boss room, like a correct answer (review, owner call 2026-11-07). The effect still lands.
  - A room the host picked the question for (指定下一題) never draws an event, so the pick is not burned.
  - The next-answer effects (curse, blessing, 凶) last until the team's next correct answer, room or boss. A wrong answer does not use them up. A new effect replaces an older one. The curse does not stack with a persuasion natural 1: the base is halved at most once. The blessing stacks with a crit 20. The kill's 4d20 is never changed. The TV and the phone show 「💀 詛咒：下次答對減半」 or 「✨ 祝福：下次答對多一半」 over the team's question.
  - The chef's 15% applies to satiety an event gives, not to what it takes. Satiety never goes below 0.
  - An event turn has no abilities: no sneak attack, warrior roll, mage hint, persuasion check or prayer.
  - 指定下一題 for a team in an event room deals when the team walks out.
  - Game log (#155): each team's events and outcomes, to tune the chance and the numbers.
  - A room restart keeps the drawn event and its outcome; nothing is redrawn. Like an answer reveal, the outcome screen itself is memory only: a restart after the press shows the next turn, with the effect already applied.

## Engineering
- **State** (`lib/game-state.ts`): `Team.event?: { scene, outcome }`. scene ∈ chest, spring, idol, fairy, fortune; outcome ∈ feast, rotten, spring, curse, blessing, great, good, bad. Both are drawn together when the team enters the room, so a restart cannot reroll them. `Team.boon?: "curse" | "blessing"` holds the next-answer effect. The broadcast `SpectatorView` carries the scene only, never the outcome, until the press. Absent fields read as no event, so saves from before this change load.
- **Draw in the room, not in `answerQuestion`.** After a `correct-room-advance` that `eventEligible` allows, `DungeonRoom` rolls `rng() >= 1 - EVENT_CHANCE` (the top of the range, so the room tests' `() => 0` never draws one) and calls `enterEventRoom(game, teamId, drawEvent(rng))`. `answerQuestion` takes no extra rng call, so every existing deterministic test keeps its deals. In e2e (`E2E_EVENT_TEAM` worker var), a team with that exact name always draws an event and every other team never does, so the existing e2e specs see no events.
- **Press** (`openEvent(game, teamId)`): applies the effect, counts the turn (turns, round), moves the team to the next room with a fresh deal (its held question passed as `own`), clears `event`, and advances the turn, like `answerQuestion`. It returns an outcome; the room holds it as `eventReveal` (a pause like `reveal`) until 下一題.
- **Guards:** a team with `event` set cannot answer, lock, submit, check or use an ability (`InvalidActionError("event_room")`). Host judge buttons are replaced by the event panel. `sendToBoss` clears `event`.
- **Messages:** client `TEAM_OPEN_EVENT { eventId }` from the turn team's phone; host `HOST_OPEN_EVENT { teamId, eventId }`. The STATE gains `eventReveal`; the question is null while the turn team stands in an event room.
- **Art** (`lib/pixel-art.ts`): new sprites chest-closed, chest-open, rotten dish, spring, idol, fairy, fortune teller, lot. 美味料理 shows a random dish sprite that already exists.
- **Sound:** cue `correct` on the press, and `fumble` on a bad outcome (rotten, curse, 凶). Both are cues that already exist.

## Success Criteria
- At 25%, roughly one team in four meets an event per room entry, verified by a unit test over many draws.
- Each outcome changes exactly what the table says, chef and floor included (unit tests).
- A snapshot saved with a team in an event room restores it with the same event; nothing is redrawn.
- No event in the boss room, and none on entering the boss room or the last room.
- e2e (CI only): the event team opens its event from the phone, the TV and the phone show the outcome, 下一題 passes the turn, and the team's next turn is a monster.

## What I noticed about how you think
- "改成直接演示事件 例如出現商人跟他對話 或者撿到寶箱": you turned a card UI into a scene. The feature got its identity there.
- "增加飽足感的應該要是吃了好吃的料理": you kept the events inside the game's own fiction, where satiety is food.
- "花飽足感實在不太合理" and then "商人不收東西就給感覺就不像商人了": you rejected both easy versions of the merchant instead of keeping a half-right one.
