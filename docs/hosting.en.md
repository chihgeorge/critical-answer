# Hosting guide

What the host needs to know on the day of the event: opening a room, running the game, and how to recover when something goes wrong. For the rules themselves, see the [full rules](rules.en.md).

## Opening a room and the lobby

The host presses 「主持遊戲」 (host a game) on the home page and lands on the host page (`/room/<code>/host`). The big-screen URL is at the very top of the host page; open it on a TV or projector (`/room/<code>/screen#s=…`). The URL carries the room's screen key (the part after `#s=`), so open all of it: click the link, copy the link address (the text shown leaves the key out because players can see the host's phone, so typing it opens a big screen with no video), or press 「顯示大螢幕 QR」 (show the big-screen QR) under the URL and scan it with the device that drives the TV or projector (a TV remote cannot type a URL this long). The QR holds the key and is for the big screen only: it stays closed until you ask for it, so press 「收起大螢幕 QR」 (hide the big-screen QR) after the scan, and do not let a player scan it. Once open, the big screen remembers the key and removes it from the address bar (the address bar is projected too), so a reload in the same browser needs no second paste. A big screen opened with only `/room/<code>/screen` still shows the whole game, but the room does not treat it as the big screen: it plays no video on a YouTube question (the top of the lobby, and the place where the video would be, say 「請用主持人頁上的大螢幕網址開啟」, open the big-screen URL on the host page), and the host page's narration panel does not show which line it is reading. The URL stays the same after the host page reloads.
Each team joins by entering a team name and the 4-character room code on a phone, or by scanning the QR code in the lobby. A team name cannot match a team that has already joined; names that differ only in upper/lower case or full-width/half-width characters count as the same name. While waiting, the phones show every team that has joined. In the host page's lobby, each team has 「改名」 (rename; same rules as joining) and 「移除」 (remove): a removed phone shows 「主持人把你們的隊伍移除了」 (the host removed your team), it is not added back by reconnecting on its own, and it can join again only by going back to the home page and opening the room again. The host presses 「開始遊戲」 (start game).

**Late teams**: a team that joins after the game started does not walk straight in. Its phone shows 「等主持人讓你們加入…」 (waiting for the host to let you in), and under the team rows the host page shows 「🚪 <team name> 想中途加入」 (wants to join late) with 「收這隊」 (let this team in) and 「不收」 (turn away). 「收這隊」 starts the team in the first room, last in turn, with the same scores as the team in last place at that moment (see the [full rules](rules.en.md#turns-and-question-banks)); 「不收」 makes that phone show 「主持人這場沒有收你們的隊伍」 (the host did not let your team into this game), and it must open the room again from the home page to wait again. The row does not show while the how-to-play pages or the initiative roll are on; let the team in once they are over. The buttons are off for the second or two a door is opening. Once a team is in the boss room only 「不收」 is left, and the waiting teams enter the lobby on their own at 「回到大廳」 (back to the lobby). Waiting teams are still there after a server restart.

Players can add the phone page to their home screen (iPhone: Safari's Share button → Add to Home Screen; Android: Chrome's menu → Add to Home screen or Install app). Opened from the icon, it runs full screen with no address bar, so it is harder to close by mistake; an icon added from the room's phone page opens straight back into that room. On an iPhone the home-screen version does not share data with Safari, so add it from the home page before joining a team, then join from the icon. A team that already joined in Safari counts as a new phone when opened from the icon. In the lobby it is refused for using a taken name: the host removes the old team with 「移除」, then the players join again from the icon with the same name. Once the game has started, take the team back with [moving a team to a new phone](#when-things-go-wrong) below.

When a room difficulty has fewer questions than teams, a warning shows above 「開始遊戲」: the same game may repeat questions or deal questions from another difficulty, and 「開始遊戲」 asks for one more confirmation. The bank editor shows the same warning above 「儲存全部變更」 (save all changes), sized for 8 teams, but does not block saving.

While waiting for people, the middle of the big screen is a campfire; as each team joins, its character runs in to stand around the fire. The room code and the join QR code are here too.

## How-to-play slides

Above 「開始遊戲」 is 「開始前播放玩法說明」 (show how to play before starting). It is checked by default in a new room, and unchecked by default after 「回到大廳，再玩一場」 (back to the lobby, play again). When it is checked, the big screen first shows 4 pages of explanation (taking turns, answering on the phone, what happens on a wrong answer, how to win), and the host page has speaker notes for each page. Press 「下一頁 ▶」 (next page) (or keyboard → / Enter; ← goes back a page) to turn pages; on page 4, the initiative roll starts only after 「開始冒險 ▶」 (start adventure), and the first question, the opening sound and the narration come once it is done. While the slides play, the phones show 「看大螢幕 👀」 (look at the big screen) and the current page number; when the big screen's voice is on, it reads the owl's line. See [docs/designs/how-to-play.md](designs/how-to-play.md).

**Initiative roll**: after the slides (or after 「開始遊戲」 when the slides are unchecked), each team rolls a d20 on its phone to set the turn order, and the big screen lines up every team's die with a countdown. The host page shows how many teams have rolled; 「🎲 全部代擲」 (roll for everyone) rolls at once for every team that has not rolled yet, and a team that has not rolled after 15 seconds is rolled for. Once the order is set, the big screen holds it for about 4 seconds before the first question. Details in the [full rules](rules.en.md#turns-and-question-banks).

When the initiative roll starts, the big screen first plays about 2 seconds of stone doors opening. The first time the boss room comes up, it plays about 2.5 seconds of a 「首領出現！」 (the boss appears!) alarm banner, with a siren when 🎵 music is on. The moment the first team enters the boss room (on a correct answer or sent in by the host), it also plays about 2.5 seconds of a purple 「⚔️ 魔王門開了！」 (the boss door is open!) last-sprint banner naming the blessed teams, with a fanfare when 🎵 music is on (see the [full rules](rules.en.md#progress-points-and-the-boss-room)). The question and narration underneath carry on as usual. With the computer's reduce-motion setting on, each shows one still frame instead. A big screen opened or reconnected mid-game does not replay them.

## Manual pacing

At the top of the host page, between the room code and 「⚙ 更多」, is the 「自動｜⏸ 手動」 (auto | manual) switch. It works at any time, in the lobby too. The default is auto: when a pause runs out, the game moves on by itself.

In manual, none of the pauses below ends by itself. The host page shows a dashed panel, 「⏸ 停在這裡：接下來是……」 (held here: next is ...), and the game goes on only when the host presses 「▶ 下一步」 (next step; keyboard `1` or Enter):

- Initiative roll: no countdown while the teams roll, and 「下一步」 rolls for the teams that have not; once the order is up, one more press brings the first question.
- The reveal after 「公布答案」, the crit die, the persuasion check, the warrior's die and the kill dice, a boss miss, and the boss's phase card.
- A fork: no countdown, the team picks in its own time. 「下一步」 is the same as time running out and opens the monster room's door. Once the door is open, one more press enters the room.
- The podium after the game: 「下一步」 starts the reveal. Once started it plays to the end, and the host turns the epilogue's pages as before.

The answer countdown is not affected: it still counts in manual. On a switch back to auto, the held step runs its usual time again from the start (a fork counts a fresh 15 seconds). The mode is saved with the room and survives a server restart. Design in [docs/designs/manual-pacing.md](designs/manual-pacing.md).

## The big screen

**Live map**: during the game, the top of the big screen is a dungeon map strip (it replaces the old team cards). Each team is a small token marked with the first character of its name, and moves into the next room on a correct answer; after the leading team there is fog, so you cannot see how many rooms are left. The boss room starts as a closed door; it opens when the first team goes in, showing the boss and its health bar. The token of the team whose turn it is has a ▼ above it; offline teams show 「zz」, and a team holding a blessing or a curse has a ✨ or 💀 on its tag's top-right corner. The map shows no scores. Design in [docs/designs/live-map.md](designs/live-map.md).

**Dungeon master (narration)**: after each judgment, the owl wizard first says the result, then calls the next team; it also makes a special announcement when a team enters the boss room. In about three lines out of ten the narrator talks like a food-show host or a biologist and judges, with a straight face, how good a monster would taste (when a monster or the boss comes on, when a monster is beaten, when a dish is eaten); a wrong answer, a flee and a die roll keep the adventure tone, scripted lines never put two food lines one after the other (a chest that holds a dish is the exception), and the AI narrator is asked to do the same. Teams whose turn it is not see on their phones whose turn it is, which monster they face, their own reward points and the same narration line, and can tap 👏😱😂👎 to float that emoji up behind the big screen's stage (see the [full rules](rules.en.md#audience-reactions)). When the game ends, the narration first hands out honor titles one at a time (every team gets at least one), then calls the places from third to first while the big screen fills the podium one place at a time, followed by an epilogue (see the [full rules](rules.en.md#winning-and-playing-again)). When the epilogue is too long for one page on the big screen, it is split into pages: the host page shows 「◀ 上一頁」 (previous page), 「下一頁 ▶」 (next page) and 「後記 1／2」 (epilogue 1/2), and the host turns the pages; when the big screen's voice is on, it reads each page as it turns. When the boss is defeated, it waits for the finishing blow's 4 dice to stop; if the boss has a dish, the big screen also first cooks the boss in the middle and serves tonight's main course, and starts calling the places only about 4.6 seconds later. When a correct answer defeats a monster with a dish, the host page first shows 「🍲 上菜」 (serve, key `1`); the big screen cooks the dish only when you press it, and the next press goes to the next question (see the [full rules](rules.en.md#monsters-into-dishes)). Narration uses scripted lines by default; with an API key it can be written live by Claude instead (see [AI narration](#ai-narration) below). In the bottom-right corner of the big screen is a voice switch (it reads the narration aloud with the browser's built-in Web Speech API). It is off by default, and its state is saved on that device; browsers without the speech API do not show this button. Next to it is a 「🎵 音樂」 (music) switch: when it is on, the big screen plays background music synthesized by the program, and judgments have sound effects too (the music lowers on its own while the narration is read aloud). It is also off by default and saved on that device.

**YouTube questions**: videos play only on the big screen, and only on one opened with the big-screen URL on the host page: the room sends the video only to a big screen that has the screen key and to the host page. A player's phone is never told which video it is, so nobody can look up its title on YouTube for the answer (see [docs/designs/screen-video-key.md](designs/screen-video-key.md)). In the host page's panel for the team on turn there are three buttons, ▶ 播放 (play), ⏸ 暫停 (pause) and ⟲ 從頭 (from the start); the green one shows your last command, ▶ or ⏸ (after ⟲, ▶ turns green). ⟲ always plays the clip again from its start. While a video plays, the background music goes silent and any narration being read stops. When a music question's answer is revealed and it has no image or video explanation, the big screen removes the pixel card and shows the picture, with the video stopped where it was; press ▶ to carry on. Browsers only let a page play a video with sound after someone has tapped it: while nobody has tapped the big screen, the lobby says 「點一下大螢幕」 (tap the big screen) at the top, so tap it once before the game starts. When the big screen cannot play a video (blocked, removed or no network), the host page shows a red warning; in a buzzer phase, play the song from your own phone or speaker and press 「🎤 現場放歌，開放搶答」 (play it live, open buzzing), and the big screen says 「主持人現場放歌」 (the host plays it live) (see [docs/designs/boss-music-buzzer.md](designs/boss-music-buzzer.md)). On a music question with answer fields (song title and singer, say), once a team buzzes, 「答對／答錯」 (correct / wrong) turns into one row per field: what the team typed shows next to the field name, press ✓ or ✗ on each, and 「送出判定」 (send the ruling) works once every field is marked. Keyboard shortcuts do not judge such a question; use the buttons on screen. If you marked every field wrong by mistake, 「↩ 剛才的某某隊重新逐欄判定」 (judge that team field by field again) takes you back to the rows (see [docs/designs/multi-field.md](designs/multi-field.md)). Design in [docs/designs/youtube-questions.md](designs/youtube-questions.md).

**Screen stays awake**: while the host page and the big screen are open, they ask the screen to stay on (Screen Wake Lock), and ask again after you switch away and come back. If the browser does not support it, refuses on low battery, or the page is not on HTTPS (for example an `http://` URL in a LAN test game), the screen sleeps as usual.

## AI narration

By default the narration is scripted: no key, no cost. In the lobby's 「AI 旁白」 (AI narration) area the host picks one of:

- **No AI**: scripted narration (default).
- **Use my own Anthropic API key**: paste your own key and press 「驗證並使用」 (verify and use). The server first calls Anthropic once to confirm the key is valid, and only then turns it on. The key is saved in this browser (localStorage) only if you check 「記住這台電腦」 (remember this computer); otherwise you must paste it again after a reload. Use a dedicated key, and set a spending limit in the Anthropic Console.
- **Owner key**: enter the site owner's `OWNER_KEY` to use the server's own `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` instead. This works only when both are set on the server.

Each room allows at most 300 AI narration lines by default (the host can change this, up to 5000). When they run out, the host is asked whether to raise the limit or switch back to scripted lines.

The host's key is kept only in the room's memory: it is never written to a snapshot, never logged, and never sent back to any screen; the host page shows only its last 4 characters (see [SECURITY.en.md](../SECURITY.en.md)). After a server restart it must be pasted again. The owner key is read only on the server (the Worker); do not give it a `NEXT_PUBLIC_` prefix, or it ends up in the browser. Narration uses `claude-haiku-4-5`, and the end-of-game epilogue uses `claude-sonnet-5-5`. Each narration line waits at most 3 seconds (15 seconds for the epilogue); on a timeout, an error or unusable content, a scripted line is used instead. After 3 failures in a row the whole room switches to scripted lines, then tries the AI once a minute and switches back when it answers; when the key is refused (401/403), the room stays on scripted lines until a new key is applied. Team names typed by players go into the prompt only as data; questions and answers never go into the prompt; the catalog's edible part of a beaten monster (for example 「外膜」) does, so the narrator can comment on it, but the dish is named only once it is served. The prompt also carries the room's last 8 AI lines and asks the model to word things differently instead of repeating them; a server restart, or the room code passing to the next host, clears them.

> **Cost**: AI narration turns on only with the host's own key or the owner key, and each room has a line limit; for the owner key's spending limit, see the [deployment guide](deployment.en.md#keys-for-ai-narration).

Above 「答對！」 (correct) on the host page sits the 「地城主旁白」 (dungeon master's narration) panel. It usually shows one line (for example 「3 句・念到第 2 句」, 3 lines, reading line 2); open it to list every line the big screen reads this time. Press 「改」 (edit) on a line not yet read to replace it (at most 50 characters), and the big screen reads on from that line; a line already read cannot be changed. Below the lines is 「下一句這樣說」 (say this next: queue a line of at most 40 characters, which the next narration says as its second sentence; see [docs/designs/host-next-line.md](designs/host-next-line.md)). Design: [docs/designs/host-narration-edit.md](designs/host-narration-edit.md). `⚙ 更多` (more) has a narration log download (each line's event, the scores at the time, source, latency and fallback reason; it is lost after a server restart).

For the owner key and server-side settings, see the [deployment guide](deployment.en.md#keys-for-ai-narration).

## When things go wrong

**Disconnect notice**: when a phone or the big screen disconnects, it shows 「連線中斷，重新連線中…」 (connection lost, reconnecting…). When a phone reconnects, it tells the team whether it was skipped while offline, or how many questions were judged in that time.

**Cannot connect, or refused to join**: if a phone, the big screen or the host page has not connected within 10 seconds of its first try, it shows 「連不上伺服器：請檢查網路，或稍後再試。」 (cannot reach the server: check the network, or try again later) and a 「重試」 (retry) button. When the room refuses a phone, the phone gives the reason in one sentence (the team name cannot be used, the team name is already taken, the room is full, the team data saved in the browser does not match, the password is wrong or required, too many wrong passwords, the team was removed or not let in by the host), with a link: password problems go back to the password entry, everything else to the home page.

**Moving a team to a new phone**: after the game starts, if a phone runs out of battery, or the team switched browsers or used a private tab, each team on the host page has 「交給新手機」 (hand to a new phone). Pressing it shows a 4-character pairing code (if the team is still online, the host is asked to confirm first). The new phone opens the room URL (any team name will do; a name already in use is refused, and that screen has the same input), enters the pairing code at the bottom of the waiting screen at 「換了手機？輸入主持人給的配對碼：」 (changed phones? enter the pairing code from the host:), and presses 「接手隊伍」 (take over team); it then rejoins as this team, with its scores and turn intact. A pairing code works only once and is valid for 5 minutes; pressing the button again gives a new code. After 5 wrong entries in the room, every pairing code still valid is cancelled, and the host must press the button again. The old phone can still play for the team if it comes back. Design in [docs/designs/team-handoff.md](designs/team-handoff.md).

**When the page breaks**: when a page crashes, it shows 「畫面出錯了」 (something went wrong on screen) and a 「重新載入」 (reload) button instead of a blank white page; reloading goes back to the game, with scores and progress intact. Each crash sends a one-line report to the Worker (`POST /api/client-error`); in the Worker log it is JSON like `{"event":"client_error",…}`.

## Known limits

- **Unused rooms rest, then are forgotten**: when a room has had no state-changing action for 2 hours (joining, starting, judging, editing narration and so on; downloading the narration log and audience reactions do not count), it closes every connection with close code 4001, and the game progress is kept. All three screens show 「房間休息中」 (room is resting) and **no longer reconnect on their own**; press 「重新連線」 (reconnect) to rejoin the same game. This keeps a TV left on from being billed all night (the Cloudflare free plan has 13,000 GB-s per day; one room open for a full 24 hours uses about 83%). Automatic reconnects do not count as actions, so a TV left on does not keep a room from ever resting.
- **A room lasts at most 6 hours**: 6 hours after its first connection, the room is wiped and its code released, even while a game is on. Screens still connected get close code 4002 and show 「房間已過期：已到房間的使用時間…」 (room expired: its time is up), and reconnecting leads to a brand-new empty lobby. With 15 minutes left, the top of the host page shows 「房間再 15 分鐘關閉」 (the room closes in 15 minutes) and a 「延長 1 小時」 (extend 1 hour) button; player phones and the big screen show nothing. Each press moves the closing time 1 hour past the current one, and the warning comes again 15 minutes before the new end. It can be pressed again and again, up to 24 hours from the room's creation; then the button shows 「已達上限」 (limit reached). A server restart does not restart the clock. The times can be changed with the environment variables `ROOM_IDLE_MS` and `ROOM_EXPIRE_MS` (milliseconds). If a room created with 「主持遊戲」 on the home page happens to hit a code still used by someone else, the host page switches to a new code on its own; opening a link to an existing room never changes the code.
- **Host identity rests on a key**: when a room is created, the browser makes a key and puts it in the URL's `#k=`. The first device to claim the room with it becomes the host, and the same key can later take over from another device. The only protection is this key plus the 4-letter room code, so **do not give the host URL to players**.
- **Team identity**: team IDs are public (every state broadcast carries them), so each team also has a secret key kept only in its own browser, which it must show when reconnecting. Someone who gets a team ID still cannot pose as that team. Rooms have no password by default: anyone who knows the 4-character code can join (at most 8 teams; one connection plays for one team; at most 60 connections per room, and a full room first closes its oldest spare connection, never the host, the first two big screens opened with the host page's URL or QR, or a team's only connection). The host can set a **join password** in the lobby; after that, new teams must enter the password to join, and teams that already joined do not need it to reconnect. The whole room takes at most 10 wrong passwords a minute; past that, every join that needs the password is refused for the rest of that minute (the phone shows 「密碼猜錯太多次，請等一分鐘再試」, too many wrong passwords, wait a minute); setting a new password lifts it at once. A script therefore needs hours to guess a short password. Set a password for a public URL. Each connection may send at most 10 messages a second (up to 30 in a burst); the rest are dropped, which normal tapping never reaches. A single message over 4096 bytes is dropped the same way (the largest normal message is about 2.5 KB).
- Monster art: the catalog has 20 room monsters and 3 bosses (the little black dragon of the abyss, the deep-sea king squid and the undying fire bird), each with its own dish picture; the default bank draws its monsters fresh each game: a different room monster in each room and any of the 3 bosses (「每場隨機抽怪」 in the bank editor). When one room monster is put in several rooms, a repeat appearance gets a new colour, an accessory and a title on its nameplate (see the [question bank guide](question-bank.en.md#default-question-bank)).
- At most 8 teams. With many teams and many rooms, the big screen's live map folds the earlier rooms into the entrance cell at the far left so the map fits on screen; this has not been tested with real people yet.
- Only a simple single-session use is supported at a time; there is no design for several games running in parallel.
