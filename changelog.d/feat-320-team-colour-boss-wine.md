### Added
- In the lobby, each team picks its team colour on the phone (#320): eight swatches, each the team's own hero in that colour. A colour another team already took is locked and shows that team's name, and the first pick wins. A joining team gets the lowest free colour, so nobody has to pick, and the colour is fixed once the game starts. A team waiting to join late can pick too. The big-screen lobby, the map pawns, the turn ribbons, the timing screen, the fortune and award cards and the podium all draw each team in its own colour.

### Changed
- The boss is now wine (`--boss`, `#8e1f3a`) instead of red, so the red team never looks like the boss. This covers the boss buzz band, the host ribbon during the boss fight, the map's boss room and HP bar, the boss hearts, its name and 暴走 chip, the 「地城主」 tag in the boss room, the phase-card and 「首領出現！」 titles, the entrance band, the rage outline and the heartbeat and parry edges. Wrong answers, alarms, countdowns and the map's turn marker stay red. The hearts after a team eats a dish stay bright red.

### Fixed
- Removing a team in the lobby no longer changes the colour of every team after it: a team's colour is its own now, not its place in the list.
