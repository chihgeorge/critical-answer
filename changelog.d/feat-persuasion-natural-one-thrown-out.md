### Changed
- A natural 1 on a fill-in question's persuasion check now throws the team out (被轟出門), the mirror of a 20. The question never shows, it scores nothing, and the team flees to a new monster in the same room, the same as 「逃跑・換房」: the question drops one difficulty and the flee counts. The turn then passes to the next team. The big screen, the team's phone and the host page show 「🗣️ 擲出 1！{怪物}把{隊名}轟出門了」 for 2 seconds. The card does not hold in 手動 pacing. The old rule, where a correct answer paid only half, is gone.
- A throw-out is not an answer, so the cleric cannot pray, the 🍀 lucky die stays with the team, and there is nothing to 改判. The host fixes the score with 修正. The next team's check does not wait the 5 seconds.
- The narration has three new scripted lines and an AI prompt for the throw-out. The tutorial owl, the dice chip and the rules (both languages) say 「擲到 1 被轟出門」.
