### Changed
- Multi-field music questions in the buzzer phase (for example song title and singer) now score each field on its own. A team that gets some of the fields right takes one heart per right field and that share of the reward right away, the answer is revealed, and the team picks the next song's category, just like an all-right answer. The host no longer has to judge a partly right answer as all right to give it points. All wrong is still an ordinary wrong buzz. The second round and the host page's partly-right list are gone.
