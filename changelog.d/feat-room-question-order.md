### Added
- Normal rooms now deal their questions in bank order: within the difficulty a room needs, the first question not asked yet, skipping one another team holds. A new lobby setting 「出題順序」 (照題庫順序 / 隨機, bank order by default) switches back to the random draw; it is fixed once the game starts and kept across a restart. A game saved before this setting keeps drawing at random.
