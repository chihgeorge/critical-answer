### Added
- 🔮 The oracle and 🚨 catches now work in the boss room while teams take turns (#289). An oracle halves the hit's points and the boss still loses its heart; it cannot be asked on the finishing blow (the last phase with one heart left), and if an oracle hit defeats the boss anyway from more hearts (a crit, a sneak attack, a music question's fields), the kill's 4d20 is halved too. The oracle goes with the shared boss question, so a team that gets the question after a skipped turn is halved too. An upheld catch is the boss room's flee: the shared boss question's answer is shown and the question changes. Neither works in a buzzer phase or while a boss phase card is due.
