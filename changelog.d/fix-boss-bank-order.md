### Changed
- Boss questions now come in bank order, whatever their difficulty: each boss phase deals its questions exactly as the bank lists them (questions the last game revealed still go last). Difficulty still sets what a hit pays. With 首領題隨機順序 on, a phase is still shuffled within a difficulty and dealt easiest first.
- The bank editor lists each boss phase in bank order again, so the list is the play order; 第 N 題 follows it everywhere, and ↑/↓ move a boss question across difficulties again. The difficulty and category filter chips stay.
