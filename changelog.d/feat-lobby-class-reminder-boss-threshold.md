### Added
- The host picks the boss threshold in the lobby: 「進王門檻」 10, 12 or 15 progress points (15 stays the default). A lower one makes a game with many teams shorter. The room keeps it in the game, so a server restart keeps it, and it cannot change once the game starts. The host page's progress, its meter and the 「⚙ 更多」 send-to-boss line show the chosen threshold.
- Teams without a class get a reminder, never a forced pick: the host lobby marks them 「未選職業」, 開始遊戲 asks 「還有 N 隊沒選職業」 in the same confirm as the small-bank warning, and the phone lobby shows one line under 「不選」.
