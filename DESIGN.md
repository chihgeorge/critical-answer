# Design system: Critical Answer (致命答題)

Cute pixel dungeon. Approved in `docs/designs/critical-answer-iteration-2.md` (design review, 2026-09-19).
Everything is drawn in code or CSS. No image assets. Sound follows the same rule: the big screen's background music and event sound effects are synthesized with the Web Audio API (`lib/bgm.ts`, `lib/sfx.ts`, `lib/bgm-player.ts`), no audio files.

|-------|-------|-----|
| `--k` | `#2a1533` | 4px outline, chip background |
| `--ink` | `#3a2245` | text on panels |

| `--danger` | `#ff6b6b` | ribbon, boss |

| `--orange` | `#e8862a` | **only** on the dark chip or large shapes |

| `--wrong` | `#c94848` | the ✕ 答錯 label and a wrong tile's bevel |

Small orange text on cream is 2.4:1 and fails. Small labels (第 N 題, 難度) use `LabelChip`, which is orange on `--k` (6.3:1).

## Type

- Chinese and body: **俐方體11號 (Cubic 11)**, `public/fonts/Cubic_11.woff2`, SIL OFL. Taiwan glyph forms.
- Digits and scores: **Press Start 2P**, class `px-num`.
- Both are bundled so LAN play works with no internet.

## Rules

- Big screen (`/screen`): a fixed **960×540** stage (every approved mockup is this size), scaled with `transform: scale()` and letterboxed outside 16:9. 1920×1080 is exactly 2x and 4K exactly 4x, so pixels stay square. (An earlier note said 1920×1080; decision 6A was revised on 2026-09-20.)
- Judge buttons live in the thumb zone. `PixelButton.ok` is 78px tall.
- Phone width (the host page and `/play`, 360px and up): under 400px the turn ribbon is 22px, the font's 2x, so a team name keeps its room after 「首領戰　輪到：」. A label and its value break between the two, never inside the value: the value is an `inline-block` (the hold panel's 「⏸ 停在這裡：」, 「大螢幕正在揭曉：」, the fork card). A host team row puts the name and numbers on the first row and the buttons on the second. Centred status lines use `text-wrap: balance`.
- Motion is two-frame `steps()` only. `prefers-reduced-motion` stops blinking, typing, hopping and cloud drift. One exception: when a map pawn changes room, the room it lands in keeps its single `--turn2` outline flash, so no team moves unseen (live map D13). The cooking after 🍲 上菜 is hard cuts between frames (monster, pot, smoke, dish, three bites, empty plate), never a slide; the pot's bubbles and the eater's chew flick between two canvases like the torches (`docs/designs/monster-cuisine.md`, cooking I8; `docs/designs/eat-dish.md`). Reduced motion shows the empty plate at once.
- Waiting teams' reactions (👏😱😂👎, `docs/designs/audience-reactions.md`) climb the big screen at z 2, behind the torches, tiles, panels, clock and dialogue box, in `steps(12)`; reduced motion holds each still for its 2.4 s. On the phone they are one row of four 76px buttons above the dialogue box that go D5 grey for 2 s after a tap.
- Sound unlock (`docs/designs/immersion.md` PR0): while the big screen's sound is not ready, the lobby shows one `--warn` `PixelButton` centred at top 250, at most 40px tall, between the room-code panel and the heroes: 「點一下開啟音效與氣氛」 over a 12px 「音樂・音效・旁白語音」, accessible name 「開啟音效與氣氛」 (never 音樂 or 語音, the toggles' names). Mid-game, while the AudioContext is asleep, a `--warn` 「👆 開聲音」 sits left of 🎵 in the bottom-right toggle cluster, same size. Both read 開啟中… on a press and 再點一次 if still asleep 1 s later. The host page's warning is one `--warn` line in the top status stack with a 44px 「知道了」; the ready line 「🔊 大螢幕聲音已就緒」 is a `lav` `Panel` for 3 s.
- Boss fight effects (`docs/designs/immersion.md` PR1 ①–④), all hard cuts or two-frame `steps()`. Entrance (`TransitionOverlay` kind `boss`, `px-boss-enter-*`): a 75% black tint for 0.5 s, then 45% for the rest of its 2.5 s, and one 190px see-through band (30% black, so the question and tiles under it stay readable) bordered in `--danger` at top 165 with the boss's own `Sprite` as a black silhouette (4px `--danger` outline) that cuts to full colour at 1 s, 「首領出現！」 in `.px-title-danger` and the name in `.px-title-gold` scaling 1.4 → 1 in two frames; the band shakes 8px twice. Parry on a boss wrong answer (1.4 s): only the monster moves — a steady `--warn` outline (`px-parry-charge`; a blink would be flashes outside the budget), then 🛡️ ⚔️ ✦ cut in at z 5, then the sprite cut to 1.3x shaking 8px (`px-parry-counter`) and one `--danger` edge pulse. Heartbeat (last 5 s of a boss answer clock): an edge vignette in `#b02a3a`, two frames per beat. Rage: the boss jitters 2px (`px-rage`) with a 3px `--danger` outline and a 「🔥 暴走！」 chip after the hearts or bar; its hue stays the phase's. Red has one job per effect. Every flash (heartbeat pulse, parry pulse, entrance lighting up) asks the shared flash budget (`lib/flash-budget.ts`, at most 3 per any 1 s on the whole screen); a refused one, or reduced motion, shows a static tint (`HEART_TINT`, `PARRY_TINT`) and never scales, shakes or jitters. Stacking: parry < phase card < transitions < top banners.
- Host effect pads (`docs/designs/immersion.md` PR2, `components/host-fx-row.tsx`): on the host page one cream `Panel` row right under the header, `position: sticky; top: 0` (the only sticky thing; `<main>` uses `overflow: clip` so it can stick), `role="group"` 「大螢幕加料」, two equal columns (`grid-cols-2`, 8px gap) of white `PixelButton`s at least 56px tall with 16px text: 「⚡ 雷聲」 and 「👹 低吼」. The state is in the label, not the colour: 「已送出」 for `HOST_FX_COOLDOWN_MS` after a press (both pads off meanwhile, plus a visually hidden `aria-live` line), a trailing 「🔇」 while the screen is muted, 「沒大螢幕」 with no keyed screen, 「⚡ 影片中」 / 「👹 影片中」 while STATE `clipPlaying`. In the lobby a 14px line under the row says to tap the TV first. On the big screen, ⚡ is a white edge vignette (`THUNDER_PULSE`, inset box-shadow, the middle stays clear) lit 0.2 s, one take from the shared flash budget; 👹 shakes the stage content ±6px for two `steps(1)` cycles (`px-growl`, a wrapper inside `<Stage>`, never Stage's scaled element, the edge glows or the banners), so with the boss's 2px rage jitter it stays within 8px. A refused take, reduced motion, a TransitionOverlay or another shake (parry counter, entrance band) gives the static tint instead, held 500 ms: `THUNDER_TINT` white at 20%, `GROWL_TINT` `--k` at 35% (not red: red is the boss's). The constants live in `lib/flash-budget.ts`. Stacking: the pads' edge layer sits with the boss edge glows at z 9, under transitions and the top banners.
- After a boss kill seen live, the end screen holds the winner, the podium and the epilogue for about 4.6 s while the boss cooks (cooking R8).
- Then the places are called (`docs/designs/podium-reveal.md`): the title reads 名次揭曉中…, every stand shows a ？ with no name, the score and crown wait. The narrator first hands out the titles (`docs/designs/podium-titles.md`): each is a 2.5 s beat with a centred `--turn` card (icon and title, the heroes, the team names, the reason), hard-cut to the next one and gone after the last; then third, second and first; each call fills its stand with a hard cut. A tie that changes the podium rolls d20s first in `TieRollOverlay` (the `RollOverlay` look, 150px dice, 90px for five or more). At first place the title becomes 奪得榜首 and every score, the crown and the confetti come up at once; the epilogue follows. The room keeps the reveal's clock (`lib/podium.ts`): a screen refreshed mid-reveal picks up at the current beat, one opened after it shows the podium at once, and so does reduced motion (with a still `--turn` card on the right listing each team's title icons). The phone shows 冒險結束！看大螢幕 📺 until one second after the screen's first place.
- A question's answer is never shown while it's still in play (the host judges by ear). One exception:
  a mage's fill-in hint, or a passed persuasion check's, shows the answer's first letter or number on every screen (`MageHint`, issue #106 D4). Once a
  team answers CORRECTLY, the answer (and optional explanation media) is shown to `/screen` and `/play`
  — this is a shared-screen, in-person party game, so everyone in the room already sees the reveal
  together; it is not a competitive-integrity boundary. See `docs/designs/question-media-content.md`'s
  D2/D3 (2026-09-23, revised the earlier blanket rule). **Note (D7, impl change):** the reveal is
  carried on `STATE` with a `reveal: AnswerReveal | null` field, not a separate `ANSWER_REVEALED`
  broadcast; it stays on screen until the host presses 「下一題」 via `HOST_NEXT` (see `lib/protocol.ts`).
  `HOST_NEXT` carries the STATE's eventId, and the room ignores any `HOST_NEXT` with a mismatched eventId (a stale second-device tap).
  A `multipleChoice` question is judged by the team's lock, not by the host's 答對 / 答錯: the lock waits
  for the host's 公布答案, and a correct reveal also stays until 下一題 (`docs/designs/host-reveal.md`, v0.33;
  it replaced the 8–15 s auto-close of `docs/designs/phone-answering.md` D10, E2–E4).
