# Design system: Critical Answer (致命答題)

Cute pixel dungeon. Approved in `docs/designs/critical-answer-iteration-2.md` (design review, 2026-09-19).
Everything is drawn in code or CSS. No image assets. Sound follows the same rule: the big screen's background music and event sound effects are synthesized with the Web Audio API (`lib/bgm.ts`, `lib/sfx.ts`, `lib/bgm-player.ts`), no audio files.

## Type

- Chinese and body: **俐方體11號 (Cubic 11)**, `public/fonts/Cubic_11.woff2`, SIL OFL. Taiwan glyph forms.
- Digits and scores: **Press Start 2P**, class `px-num`.
- Both are bundled so LAN play works with no internet.

## Rules

- Big screen (`/screen`): a fixed **960×540** stage (every approved mockup is this size), scaled with `transform: scale()` and letterboxed outside 16:9. 1920×1080 is exactly 2x and 4K exactly 4x, so pixels stay square. (An earlier note said 1920×1080; decision 6A was revised on 2026-09-20.)
- Stone frames and wooden boards (direction D 「石框木牌」, 2026-11-16, research in `~/.gstack/projects/chihgeorge-critical-answer/reports/dungeon-art-research-2026-11-16.md`, local only). The big screen's question panel (`Panel` with `px-stone`, also on the event-room and fortune screens) and its owl `DialogueBox` (every non-`compact` box gets `px-stone`) wear a stone frame outside their outline: 4px ink, 4px stone lit top-left and shaded bottom-right, 2px ink, then a 4px drop; six 4px iron rivets sit on the band, two on the top and bottom 12px in from the corners and one mid-left and mid-right. It is all `box-shadow` and one `::before`, outside the box, so padding, text wrapping and the question's fit are unchanged; the frame reaches 6px lower than the plain panel and a tile's ink ring stands 3px above the tile, so `/screen` fits the question 9px higher while the frames are on (`STONE_REACH`). Every `.px-opt` tile (McOption on `/screen` and `/play`, the sort rows, the class picker) is a wooden board: its 4px border keeps its width and shows the wood (lit top, shaded bottom, an iron nail mid-left and mid-right), the ink outline is a 3px ring outside it, and the letter chip is an iron plate. Faces, text colours and labels are unchanged for every look, so each state still reads by face plus label: `plain` cream, `sel`/`bad` red with ◀ 已選 or ✕, `lock` yellow with a `--turn2` outline over the wood and 🔒, `ok` green with a white outline over the wood and ✓, `dim` grey weathered wood, an `--dim-line` ring, opaque (D12). Lock, ok, focus and the picked class draw their outline over the wood band (`outline-offset: -4px`) so it never reaches the next tile. No motion and no red were added. **Revert:** delete `px-frames` from `<body>` in `app/layout.tsx`; every frame rule is scoped under that class. `/screen?frames=0` takes it off one TV without a deploy (the page puts it back when it unmounts).
- Judge buttons live in the thumb zone. `PixelButton.ok` is 78px tall.
- Phone width (the host page and `/play`, 360px and up): under 400px the turn ribbon is 22px, the font's 2x, so a team name keeps its room after 「首領戰　輪到：」. A label and its value break between the two, never inside the value: the value is an `inline-block` (the hold panel's 「⏸ 停在這裡：」, 「大螢幕正在揭曉：」, the fork card). A host team row puts the name and numbers on the first row and the buttons on the second. Centred status lines use `text-wrap: balance`.
- Motion is two-frame `steps()` only. `prefers-reduced-motion` stops blinking, typing, hopping and cloud drift. One exception: when a map pawn changes room, the room it lands in keeps its single `--turn2` outline flash, so no team moves unseen (live map D13). The cooking after 🍲 上菜 is hard cuts between frames (monster, pot, smoke, dish, three bites, empty plate), never a slide; the pot's bubbles and the eater's chew flick between two canvases like the torches (`docs/designs/monster-cuisine.md`, cooking I8; `docs/designs/eat-dish.md`). Reduced motion shows the empty plate at once.
- Waiting teams' reactions (👏😱😂👎, `docs/designs/audience-reactions.md`) climb the big screen at z 2, behind the torches, tiles, panels, clock and dialogue box, in `steps(12)`; reduced motion holds each still for its 2.4 s. On the phone they are one row of four 76px buttons above the dialogue box that go D5 grey for 2 s after a tap.

- Torch-lit dungeon (`docs/designs/dungeon-scene.md`, `drawScene` in `lib/pixel-art.ts`). The room is rough stone in four shades with cracks and gaps, a ceiling band and a shadow at the wall's foot, a deep stone arch with a raised iron portcullis (叉路: two doorways, no portcullis), chains (vines in 苔蘚水道), corner cobwebs and two skull niches beside the arch. Light is baked into the canvas once: ambient 0.6, warm pools around the two torches and a cool pool in the arch's mouth, cut into sixths with a one-pixel checker between steps (banded, never smooth); the wall between the ceiling band and the arch's crown, away from the torches, is on average no darker than the old scene under 🌑 (`lib/scene-classic.test.ts`). The torches come from one table, `SCENE_TORCHES`, for both the sprites and the baked light: `low` beside the lobby's campfire and on every scene without a question panel, `high` (scale 4, top 186) beside the question panel, clear of the A–D tiles, through the question and its reveal. No CSS `px-glow` in this look. The biome goes by depth (`roomTheme`): rooms 1–2 stone 「入口石牆」, 3–4 moss 「苔蘚水道」 (with a water channel), 5 on deep 「幽暗深淵」 (cold blue-grey), the boss room crimson, the darkest by its palette (the wine `--boss` stays the boss's).
